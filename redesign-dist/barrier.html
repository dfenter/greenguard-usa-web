Why Human Scent Masking Changes Everything

A CO₂ trap competes with the people in your yard.

Both you and your trap emit CO₂. Both emit heat and scent compounds. Mosquitoes respond to whichever signal is strongest, and a person standing nearby often wins that competition.

The barrier removes your scent from the equation entirely. Your CO₂ trap is no longer competing. It is the only game in town. Every mosquito that enters your property follows the CO₂ plume directly into capture rather than toward your family.

The result is a measurably higher capture rate from your existing trap, and significantly less biting activity in the areas where you actually spend time.

Why CO₂ Trap Customers Only

The barrier needs a trap to work

The barrier treatment works by redirecting mosquitoes. It does not eliminate them on its own. Without a CO₂ trap present, masking human scent simply disperses mosquitoes around the property rather than capturing them. The treatment is only effective as part of a complete system.

Common Questions

Barrier treatment FAQ

Is the treatment safe for kids, pets, and bees?

Yes. The formula is entirely plant-based and free of synthetic pesticides. Children and pets can access treated areas on the same day as application with no waiting period required. The formula does not affect bees or pollinators. It is specifically designed to target the scent signals mosquitoes use to locate hosts, which bees and other beneficial insects do not respond to.

Why is the barrier only available to CO₂ trap customers?

The barrier masks human scent to redirect mosquitoes toward your CO₂ trap. Without a trap present, the barrier disperses mosquitoes around the property rather than eliminating them. The two systems are designed to work together. The barrier removes the competing signal, and the trap captures the mosquitoes that get redirected. Offering the barrier without a trap in place would not produce meaningful results for the customer.

How long does each treatment last?

Each treatment provides up to 28 days of protection under normal conditions. Heavy rain can reduce the effective window. We recommend scheduling recurring treatments every four weeks during Austin's mosquito season, which typically runs from March through November.

How is this different from a traditional mosquito spray service?

Traditional spray services apply synthetic insecticides across your entire yard. These leave residue on surfaces, carry re-entry waiting periods, and are harmful to bees and pollinators. Our treatment uses only plant-derived essential oils applied to targeted zones, with no residue, no pollinator risk, and no waiting period before your family can use the yard.

Do I need to be home for the treatment?

No. As long as your yard is accessible, our technician can complete the treatment without you being present. We will notify you when the service is complete. Most customers leave a gate unlocked on treatment day and resume normal use of the yard the same afternoon.

Is there a contract for recurring barrier treatment?

No. Like all GreenGuard USA services, the barrier treatment is month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can book a single treatment or set up a recurring schedule and cancel or pause at any time with 48 hours notice.
