What do I still need to purchase?

Just a CO₂ fill and a 9V battery for the timer. Take the tank to any welding supply, fire extinguisher service, or homebrew shop. Fill cost is typically $20 to $35. Or add our monthly CO₂ delivery service.

Why is the CO₂ tank sold empty?

US carrier regulations prohibit shipping pressurized CO₂ cylinders. The tank ships empty, which is legal and standard. Your local fill station can have it ready in minutes.

Can I add a second trap later?

Absolutely. Start with one trap and expand over time. The CO₂ timer can control up to 5 traps simultaneously from a single regulator. We offer multi-trap placement planning at no cost.
