The Science

How CO₂ trapping works

Mosquitoes find you by tracking the CO₂ you exhale. We use that against them silently, continuously, without a single pesticide.

01

CO₂ Mimics Human Breath

Our CO₂ traps emit a precise stream of carbon dioxide along with a customized scent bait, attracting mosquitoes using the exact signals humans naturally produce.

02

Mosquitoes Enter the Trap

Lured in, mosquitoes are sucked in by a fan and captured in a net. No zapping, no pesticides, no residue, just quiet, targeted control at the source.

03

We Refill. You Relax.

We deliver and install full CO₂ tanks to your property on a set schedule via our all-electric fleet. Your trap keeps running and your property stays protected.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

More questions? See all FAQs or compare all options.

How is CO₂ trapping different from mosquito sprays?

Sprays coat your yard in pesticides that kill indiscriminately, including bees and beneficial insects. CO₂ traps use the same signal mosquitoes use to find you, drawing them in and capturing them without any chemicals. No spray drift, no residue, no bee deaths.

How quickly will I see results?

Most customers notice a significant reduction in mosquito activity within 2 to 4 weeks. Studies show up to 93% bite reduction after 6 months of continuous trapping as the local breeding population collapses.

What does the service include?

We handle everything: trap installation, monthly CO₂ tank exchanges, maintenance, and seasonal adjustments. You don't touch the equipment. We show up, swap tanks, and leave your yard protected.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No contracts. Service runs month to month. Most customers stay because it works, not because they're locked in.

Is it safe for kids, pets, and bees?

Yes. CO₂ traps target only blood-seeking insects following carbon dioxide. Bees don't follow CO₂ cues, so they're completely unaffected. No pesticides means no risk to kids, pets, or your garden.
