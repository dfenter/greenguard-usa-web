Why Spraying Is the Wrong Tool

The fundamental limitation of spray-based control

Mosquito spray programs kill adult mosquitoes present at the moment of application. Within days to a week, the local population recovers as new adults emerge from breeding sites that were not treated and from neighboring properties. The cycle repeats every few weeks through the season.

This is not a failure of execution. It is a structural limitation of the spray-based approach. No spray program can address breeding sites it does not reach, and no spray program can stop mosquitoes from arriving from adjacent properties and natural areas. The population always rebounds.

What Works Instead

Continuous population suppression

CO₂ protection technology works differently. Rather than killing adults at a moment in time, it continuously removes breeding females from the local population before they can reproduce. Over two to three weeks of continuous operation, the population collapses from the inside out. Learn the science behind population suppression.

Because nothing is applied to your property, there are no residue concerns, no re-entry intervals, no neighbor notification requirements, and no waiting periods. The protection works continuously and silently, whether or not anyone is home.
