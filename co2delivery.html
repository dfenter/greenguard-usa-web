Every Visit Includes

More than just a tank swap

Every GreenGuard USA delivery visit is a full service call. Our technicians do not just drop off a tank -- they inspect, clean, and calibrate your entire system.

CO2 Tank Exchange

A full CO2 tank is connected to your regulator and the empty is removed and taken away. Your trap never stops running. Tank sizing is calibrated to your specific setup and usage pattern.

Trap Inspection and Cleaning

The technician inspects the trap for proper airflow, fan function, and catch net condition. The catch net is cleaned and reset. Any wear or component issues are flagged and addressed.

Regulator Check and Adjustment

CO2 flow rate is verified and adjusted as needed to maintain optimal trap performance. Proper regulator settings are critical to trap efficacy and CO2 tank longevity.

Attractant Replenishment

BG-Sweetscent attractant (Biogents) or Mosqilure (Mosqitter Grand) is checked and replenished as required. Attractant performance directly affects trap catch rates.

Seasonal Placement Review

As vegetation, shade patterns, and mosquito activity shift through the season, our technicians note any placement adjustments that would improve performance and discuss with you as needed.

Service Documentation

Every visit is logged. Trap performance, tank usage, and any observations are recorded so we can identify patterns and optimize your setup over time.
