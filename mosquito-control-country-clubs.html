The Member Experience Is the Product

Protecting what drives membership value

Country club members join for the outdoor lifestyle, the tennis courts, the pool, the terrace dining, the social events on the lawn. Every hour of mosquito-driven discomfort is an hour of membership value not delivered. Across a full season, the cumulative impact on member satisfaction is significant.

GreenGuard USA's outdoor protection program creates a baseline outdoor comfort that is simply present. Members do not notice it because they are not being bitten. That invisibility is the point.
