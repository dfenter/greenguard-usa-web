CO2 Trap Timer Rev 3.0

ATtiny84A · DS3231M RTC · TM1637 Display · DRV8833 Bistable Latching Solenoid · 9V Battery + DC Adapter Input · Hammond 1554CGY

Overview

The GreenGuard CO2 Trap Timer Rev 3.0 is a 24-hour programmable timer for GreenGuard CO2 mosquito traps. It drives a bistable latching solenoid with a 50 ms directional pulse at the schedule boundaries, then idles at near-zero coil current between transitions. During the OFF window, the MCU enters power-down sleep (~115 µA total); the display runs during the ON window only.

The DS3231M MEMS RTC maintains the schedule without a coin cell. Two tactile buttons allow field time-setting with 30-second auto-exit for field safety.

Default Schedule

EventTime
CO2 ON (bistable valve opens)05:30
CO2 OFF (bistable valve closes)23:30

Schedule is hardcoded in firmware. Field-adjustable only by reflashing.
