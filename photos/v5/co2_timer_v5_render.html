Enclosed components

Controller PCB — ATtiny84A + DS3231M + DRV8871, forward-mounted on 4× M3 standoffs.
2× 9 V battery — BAT1 (J1) + BAT2 (J6), diode-OR'd. Both fully internal in base-tray clips.
Bistable latching valve — wired to J2, tubing to the rear CO₂ barbs. Zero hold current.
