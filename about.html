Our Founder

Why GreenGuard USA Exists

Lauren Hughes founded GreenGuard USA in 2025 after experiencing the limitations of conventional mosquito control on their own property, and the seeing failure of mosquito sprays.

The company was built around a conviction: that the best mosquito control is the kind that targets only mosquitoes, leaves no residue in the environment, and works better over time rather than requiring constant re-application. CO₂ trapping met every criterion. The rest followed from there.

Today GreenGuard USA serves Austin, Westlake Hills, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Steiner Ranch, Round Rock, Georgetown, and surrounding communities, delivering pesticide-free mosquito protection by an all-electric EV fleet.
