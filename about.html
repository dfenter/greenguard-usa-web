Our Founder

Why GreenGuard USA Exists

Lauren Hughes founded GreenGuard USA in 2025 after years of watching families across Austin fight a losing battle against mosquitoes, including her own.

Lauren and her husband had spent more than 25 years building a life in Central Texas, raising two kids, chasing a dog through the backyard, and enjoying the outdoors that make Austin feel like home.

Over time, enjoying the backyard became harder. Mosquitoes took over summer evenings, and being outside after sunset meant constantly getting swarmed. What Lauren noticed was not just more mosquitoes, but fewer of the things families actually want around their homes. Bees, butterflies, and fireflies became harder to find. Families across Austin were spraying more and more pesticides, but the mosquitoes kept coming back while bees and fireflies disappeared.

Lauren knew there had to be a better way. That search eventually led her to CO₂ mosquito trapping technology, a system that mimics human breath to specifically attract and capture mosquitoes without pesticides, without toxic residue, and without harming pollinators or beneficial insects. For the first time, she found something that actually worked while protecting the environment instead of damaging it.

It was not easy to build from there. Running a CO₂-based system came with real logistical challenges. The tanks are heavy. Suppliers charge wildly different prices. Welding shops treat CO₂ like an industrial commodity, not the backbone of an environmentally conscious mosquito control company. But Lauren kept pushing because she believed the mission mattered.

GreenGuard USA was built around a simple conviction: mosquito control should target mosquitoes and nothing else.

Today, GreenGuard USA serves Austin, Westlake Hills, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, Steiner Ranch, Round Rock, Georgetown, and surrounding communities with pesticide-free mosquito protection delivered by an all-electric EV fleet.

For Lauren, this business was never just about pest control. It was about helping families enjoy their backyards again without harming the environment around them.
