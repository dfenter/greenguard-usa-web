    How it works

    One Mac. Your accounts. A subscription, not a meter.

    Most "AI for small business" tools resell tokens. One Person Show runs Claude through the same flat subscription you would buy for yourself, on a Mac mini that sits in your house and talks to your customer portal over an encrypted tunnel. Your data stays in your Google, Stripe and calendar. If the Mac is off, the portal keeps taking bookings and payments; the assistant simply says it will be back.

    1. Your accountsGoogle Workspace, Stripe, your calendar. Optional HubSpot. Nothing to migrate into.
    2. Your portalA customer site and an owner/tech app at your own domain, hosted for you.
    3. The closetA Mac mini running Claude on your subscription, with a daemon that reads the queue and does the work. No API bill.
    4. Your rulebookEarliest appointment, no-Saturday, service radius, who gets a text vs an email. Set once, enforced everywhere, editable any time.
