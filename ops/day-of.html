What happens

Step by step

  1. RouteStops are read from the live calendar, never a cache, ordered farthest-first and emailed to the truck at 7:30.
  2. RemindDay-before email. Two-hour text. "On my way" text from the tech’s stop card with one tap.
  3. ArriveThe stop card shows gate code, pets, access notes and the last visit’s notes. Navigate opens Maps.
  4. FinishSignature on the phone, photos checked for a proper install, services ticked. The invoice is generated from the same catalog.
  5. GuardA second finish on the same booking cannot bill twice. Thank-you email goes out at 8:00 the next morning with the next visit date.
