What happens

Step by step

  1. QuotesUnpaid quotes are nudged at 48 hours, 7 days, 14 days, then marked cold. Paid ones book an install.
  2. PaymentsA failed card is retried and the customer is emailed at day 0, 2, 7 and 14 before service pauses.
  3. ReviewsAfter the third visit, a review request with your direct link. Replies to reviews are drafted for you.
  4. Win-backEach season, lapsed customers get a short personal note written from their history.
  5. ReviewMonday 9:00: revenue, this week’s visits, open invoices, quote pipeline, new customers, system health, with what to do about each.
