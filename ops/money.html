What happens

Step by step

  1. IngestStripe charges, refunds and payouts post to the ledger every 15 minutes. Bank CSVs from Amex, Chase, Capital One or generic.
  2. CategorizeUnmatched rows are categorized by the assistant and the rule it used is saved, so next month is automatic.
  3. BriefA 7:00 morning brief: cash position, receivables at risk, churn signals.
  4. CloseOn the first: P&L, receivables aging, sales tax, mileage. Emailed to you as a package.
  5. AskAsk the books in English. The assistant writes a read-only query, runs it, and shows the answer with the SQL.
