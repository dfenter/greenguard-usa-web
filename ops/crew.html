What happens

Step by step

  1. ClockCrew clock in and out on their own timesheet. They see only their own hours and rates.
  2. ApproveYou approve time weekly. Every edit is recorded in an append-only audit trail with who and when.
  3. RunPayroll computes FLSA overtime on the blended rate, IRS percentage-method withholding, FICA caps, FUTA and state unemployment.
  4. PayPrintable pay stubs. Expense reimbursements ride along, non-taxable, once.
  5. FileDeposit schedule and amounts, quarterly 941 pre-filled as a PDF, 940 worksheet, W-2 boxes. You sign and submit.
