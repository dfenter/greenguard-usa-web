What happens

Step by step

  1. ReadNew mail is classified: scheduling, question, complaint, other. Newsletters and receipts are filed, not answered.
  2. DraftA reply is written from five sample emails you gave us at setup: your greeting, your length, your sign-off.
  3. Offer timesIf it is a scheduling request, the draft includes two real open slots from your calendar.
  4. AlertGenuine customer mail pings your phone and forwards to your tech, so nobody is the bottleneck.
  5. SendYou tap send in Gmail. Edited drafts teach nothing to anyone but you; there is no training on your mail.
Under the hood

What you get

  • Drafts only; nothing goes out without a human tap
  • Runs on your Claude subscription, not a per-message API bill
  • Thread history is included so replies make sense
  • Owner alerts by text; customer replies by email
