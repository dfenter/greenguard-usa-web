How it works

One Mac. Your accounts. A subscription, not a meter.

Your customers use a hosted portal. Your Mac does the thinking on a flat Claude subscription. Your data never leaves the Google, Stripe and calendar accounts you already own.

The pieces

Four layers

  1. Your accountsGoogle Workspace for mail and calendar, Stripe for money, optional HubSpot as the customer record, optional Cal.com for self-booking. Each stays canonical for its own data: calendar for time, Stripe for money, CRM for configuration.
  2. Your portalA customer site (quote, pay, book, plan, history, chat) and an owner/tech app (today, rounds, calendar, clients, invoices, books, payroll) at your domain, hosted and updated for you.
  3. The closetA Mac mini running the Claude command line on your own subscription. A small daemon answers portal chat with real tools, drafts your email, categorizes your books, checks install photos. No per-token bill; a flat monthly plan you control.
  4. Your rulebookA plain configuration file: earliest appointment, no-Saturday, service radius, routing order, which reminders go by text, quote follow-up windows, recurring cadence. Enforced in booking, routing and the assistant alike.
Failure modes

What happens when things break

  • Mac offline: portal, payments and bookings continue; chat says it will be back; drafts resume when the Mac returns
  • Card declined: retried and escalated on a schedule, service paused at day 14, never silently dropped
  • Booking outside your radius via a direct link: auto-cancelled with a note to you
  • A tool ran but the reply failed: the assistant never re-runs a mutation; it apologizes and flags you
Ownership

Whose data is it

  • Yours. It lives in your Google, Stripe and CRM accounts
  • The Claude subscription is on your account; we never proxy a shared key
  • Nothing is trained on your mail or customers
  • Cancel any month; nothing to export because nothing was moved
