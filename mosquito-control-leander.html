Local Mosquito Conditions

Leander Mosquito Pressure: Lake Travis Edge and Suburban Growth

Leander sits at the northern edge of Lake Travis, with the Balcones Canyonlands and Cedar Park corridor to the east. The city's proximity to Lake Travis introduces Culex pressure from the lake and its surrounding drainage, while the dense residential development that has spread across Williamson County in the past decade creates the container and irrigation-based breeding habitat that supports tiger mosquito populations. The result is sustained pressure from both off-site water sources and property-level breeding.
