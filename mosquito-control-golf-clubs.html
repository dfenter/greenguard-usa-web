Protecting the Member Experience

Why outdoor comfort is the product

Golf clubs and country clubs sell outdoor experiences. The terrace dining, the pool deck, the practice green, the 18th hole celebrations, these are the moments that define membership value. Mosquito pressure that diminishes these experiences affects both member satisfaction and the club's competitive positioning.

GreenGuard USA's outdoor protection program is designed for continuous, professional operation across large outdoor footprints. Multi-unit installations create overlapping coverage zones that protect member-facing areas without visible treatment equipment in aesthetic settings.
