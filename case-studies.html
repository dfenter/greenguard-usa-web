Challenge: A Westlake Hills homeowner with a mature live oak canopy and Barton Creek drainage at the back of the property. Severe Aedes aegypti pressure from late April through October, making the pool and patio unusable during evening hours. Previous spray treatments produced two-day relief then full return.

Solution: Single Biogents BG-Mosquitaire installed at the lawn-to-tree-line transition, oriented south facing the creek. Professional placement consultation identified a shaded drainage channel 60 feet from the patio as the primary breeding site. Within 10 days, significant reduction in patio biting. Six-week assessment confirmed 91% reduction in trap catch compared to initial counts.
