The Problem With Spray Programs for Event Venues

Why they fall short

Spray-based mosquito programs require a treatment window before events, typically 24 to 48 hours in advance. This creates scheduling dependencies that conflict with the unpredictable timeline of event preparation. It also leaves pesticide residue on outdoor furniture, linens, and food service surfaces that cannot be eliminated before guests arrive.

For venues that host multiple events per week, the spray cycle rarely aligns with the event calendar. The result is either over-treatment (spray residue at every event) or under-treatment (insufficient protection when schedules slip).

Continuous Protection, No Scheduling Required

How GreenGuard USA works for venues

GreenGuard USA's outdoor protection program runs continuously. There are no pre-event treatment windows. There is no residue on tables, chairs, or food service areas. There is nothing to notify guests about. The protection is simply in place, all the time, regardless of the event schedule.

For outdoor ceremony and reception spaces, we install Mosqitter Grand units at the perimeter of the guest area, positioned to intercept mosquitoes before they reach your guests. Units can be activated three to four hours before an event begins for maximum suppression during the event itself.
