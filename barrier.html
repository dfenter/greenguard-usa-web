The Mechanism

How the barrier and trap work as one system

The barrier treatment is not a standalone product. It is engineered to amplify the performance of your CO2 trap by removing the competing signal that draws mosquitoes toward people.

1

Property Inspection

Our technician maps mosquito pressure zones across your property, identifies resting areas and vegetation corridors, and confirms your CO2 trap placement. This determines exactly where to apply the barrier for maximum effect.

2

Scent Masking Application

We apply our plant-based essential oil formula around the perimeter of your property and key resting zones. The formula masks the human scent compounds mosquitoes rely on to locate hosts, effectively cloaking your outdoor living areas.

3

Mosquitoes Redirected to the Trap

With human scent masked across the property, your CO2 trap becomes the strongest attractant signal in the area. Mosquitoes that would otherwise target people are drawn toward the trap, captured, and removed from the breeding population continuously.

Why human scent masking changes everything

A CO2 trap competes with the people in your yard. Both emit CO2. Both emit heat and scent compounds. Mosquitoes respond to whichever signal is strongest -- and a person standing nearby often wins that competition.

The barrier removes your scent from the equation entirely. Your CO2 trap is no longer competing -- it is the only game in town. Every mosquito that enters your property follows the CO2 plume directly into capture rather than toward your family.

The result is a measurably higher capture rate from your existing trap, and significantly less biting activity in the areas where you actually spend time.

Why CO2 Trap Customers Only

The barrier needs a trap to work

The barrier treatment works by redirecting mosquitoes -- it does not eliminate them on its own. Without a CO2 trap present, masking human scent simply disperses mosquitoes around the property rather than capturing them. The treatment is only effective as part of a complete system.
