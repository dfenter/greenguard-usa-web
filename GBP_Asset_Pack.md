1. **Q: Do you use pesticides or spray chemicals?**

2. **Q: Is it safe for my pets, kids, and bees?**

5. **Q: Do I have to sign a contract?**
   A: No. Service is month to month. Stay as long as it is working for you.

6. **Q: How long until I see fewer mosquitoes?**
   A: Most customers notice meaningfully fewer mosquitoes within one to two weeks. Peak reduction typically lands around six to eight weeks as the trap removes breeding females from the cycle.
