Outdoor Wellness Is Not a Luxury

It is a baseline worth protecting

Research on human wellbeing consistently shows that time outdoors, in natural settings with vegetation, has measurable benefits for mood, stress, and cognitive function. Austin's climate makes this accessible across most of the calendar year.

The barrier to outdoor wellness for most Austin families is not the weather. It is mosquito pressure. The outdoor dinner that moves inside. The evening walk that gets cut short. The pool that sits unused through summer afternoons. GreenGuard USA was built specifically to remove that barrier, without creating new ones through pesticide exposure.
