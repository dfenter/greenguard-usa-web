The Hospitality Standard for Outdoor Protection

What guests expect

Resort guests have an expectation of comfort in outdoor spaces, pool areas, dining terraces, garden pathways, and recreation areas. Mosquito pressure that interrupts outdoor enjoyment directly affects guest satisfaction scores, reviews, and return visits. It is a hospitality problem as much as an environmental one.

The conventional spray approach creates its own hospitality problems: the chemical scent following a treatment, the residue on outdoor furniture and dining surfaces, the need to notify guests of treatment schedules. GreenGuard USA's continuous protection program eliminates all of these concerns.

Hospitality-Grade Outdoor Protection

Continuous, invisible, pesticide-free

Our outdoor protection system operates continuously without guest-visible treatment. There is no spray scent, no residue, no notification requirement, and no treatment windows to work around. The protection is built into the property environment and functions 24 hours a day.

For resort properties, we design multi-unit coverage plans that create overlapping protection zones across pool decks, outdoor dining, garden pathways, and recreation areas. The Mosqitter Grand's app control allows operating schedules to be synchronized with property activity patterns.
