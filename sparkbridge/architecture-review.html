What Happens After You Send It

StepWhat we do
1We reply to confirm what you sent and ask for anything missing. Most reviews need one short call with the engineer who owns the estate.
2GreenGuard USA engineers draw the current and recommended architectures and size them from the measured figures on the benchmark report, not from a spreadsheet of guesses.
3You receive the document. We walk through it once if you want. Where SparkBridge does not solve a problem you have, the document says so.
4If you proceed, the review becomes the deployment plan. Development use of every module is free with no time limit, so the pilot costs nothing to start.

We built the modules and run the benchmarks ourselves. The review is done by the same people, which is why it is free: it is the fastest way for both sides to find out whether the architecture fits.
