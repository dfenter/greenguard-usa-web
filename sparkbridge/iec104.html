Talk to utility equipment directly

One less box between you and your data.

This driver speaks the utility standard IEC 60870-5-104 directly from your Ignition gateway to your remote equipment. There is no intermediate translator to license, patch, monitor or replace when it fails at three in the morning.

Removing that middle step also removes a place where information gets lost. Readings arrive with the time and the fault status the equipment itself reported, rather than whatever the translator decided to pass along.

It reads only. It cannot operate a breaker or issue any control command, which keeps a data connection firmly out of the safety conversation.

The driver was tested against the reference implementation the industry uses to validate this protocol. Across a full session of 162 exchanges it made no errors, handled every interruption correctly, and shut down cleanly.

Protocol correctness is not a detail here. It is the difference between a connection that quietly drops readings and one you can build an operation on.

If you run IEC 104 equipment and would rather have one less vendor in the path between your plant and your data, this is worth a conversation. Early sites shape what ships.
