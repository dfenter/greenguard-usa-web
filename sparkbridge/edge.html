The link drops, Edge keeps recording to disk, and every reading arrives on its real time once the link is back.

Your data leaves the site once, and it leaves true.

Most sites poll: something in the middle asks the equipment for every value, over and over, whether or not anything happened. That costs more every time you add a tag or a dashboard, and it has no good answer for a dropped link.

Edge sends a value once, when it changes, stamped with the time your equipment gave it. If the link goes down, Edge keeps recording to a local buffer on disk. When the link comes back, the backlog arrives in order and lands on the time it actually happened, not the time it happened to reach the server.

Nothing Is Lost, and Nothing Is Guessed

A dropped link is not an edge case. It is Tuesday at a site on cellular or radio, and Edge is built around it rather than hoping it never happens.

Without it

The trend goes flat, then jumps

Most systems record when a value arrives. An outage leaves a hole, and the report built on that stretch of data is wrong. Somebody has to notice, explain it, and hope nobody downstream relied on it.

With Edge

The backlog fills in where it belongs

Edge keeps recording locally while the link is down. On reconnect, every reading arrives stamped with the time your equipment gave it, so the curve your team looks at is the curve the plant actually ran.

What SparkLink Saves You on a Metered Link

Included with Edge, at no extra cost. For a site on cellular or satellite, this is the part that keeps the monthly bill from being a surprise.

A remote pad, a lift station or a tank farm on a metered link pays for data by the byte. Today the only controls most sites have are settings picked by hand at commissioning, with no visibility into what a site actually spends and no way to see which single tag is burning the budget.

SparkLink measures what every site spends, attributes it down to the individual tag, and, only if spend is running ahead of plan, eases off gradually rather than all at once. Anything you mark as safety-relevant is never touched, at any spend level. You get a monthly answer to "what did this site cost," and a site that stays inside its plan without you watching it.
