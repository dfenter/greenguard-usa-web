Every Tool You Already Own Reads Your Plant Data Directly

Right now, every historian, dashboard and analytics tool that wants your plant data needs its own custom connector, built and maintained by someone, forever. Adding the next tool becomes a small project every time.

Passage opens your live plant data on a standard interface. Your historians, your SCADA, your analytics and test tools read it as is. Adding the next system becomes a subscription, not a build.

One Rate, One Total, One Efficiency Number, Calculated Once

Today the same formula for a rate, a total or an efficiency gets built three times: once in a PLC that is expensive to change, once in a dashboard nobody else can see, once in a spreadsheet that quietly drifts out of sync with the other two.

SparkCalc computes it once, in one place, on live plant data, and every system that needs the number uses the same one. Change the formula once and every consumer picks it up.

A Read Permission Never Quietly Includes Control

Access rules for a plant data network are usually hand-typed, which means a wildcard written to grant read access can silently grant control as well, and nobody notices until an audit or an incident finds it. Credentials shared between two roles cannot be rotated or revoked one at a time.

SparkID generates access rules from a plain statement of intent, so a permission does exactly what you meant and nothing more. Who can read, who can send commands, and safe rotation of credentials, without a human ever typing a wildcard.

A Score for Your Plant Data Network, With the Evidence and the Fix

Most plants cannot answer, in a sentence, how exposed their plant data network is: who can read what, what is encrypted, which identities exist and which should not. When a buyer or an auditor asks, the answer today is screenshots and tribal knowledge, assembled by hand.

Sentinel scores your plant data network, ranks the findings, and hands you the evidence and the fix for each one. It observes and changes nothing on its own. A compliance pack that turns a Sentinel run into an auditor-ready binder is a $2,495 add-on.

Every Gateway You Run, in One View

Operating forty gateways today means forty separate logins, a spreadsheet to track versions and certificates, and a rotation done by hand at every site, one at a time.

FleetOps gives you one view of every gateway: versions, drift from what you intended, certificate expiry, backup status. Fix a setting once, and apply it everywhere. Authorize a rotation once, and it runs fleet-wide.

History That Stays True Even After an Outage

Most systems record when a value arrives, not when it happened. After an outage that turns a real production curve into a flat wall of nothing, then a jump, and every report built on it is wrong.

SparkVault keeps your plant's history on the plant's own clock, in an open database your existing tools can read directly, so an outage never leaves a hole and any past day can be replayed on demand.

Your Plant Data in Your Warehouse, With No Meter Running

Getting production data into a warehouse is normally a per-row subscription and a standing worry about whether a reading was lost or counted twice during a restart.

SparkInject delivers your production data into your own warehouse continuously, once, with every reading tracked so a restart or a dropped link cannot cause a gap or a duplicate. You own the account and the bill; we are never in the middle of it.
