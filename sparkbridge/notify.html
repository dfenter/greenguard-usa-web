Situations This Solves

Each one is the same shape: equipment that already speaks a real protocol, kept out of Ignition by the lack of a native driver.

The situationWhat changes
Alarms only reach people watching an Ignition client
Any plant where the on-call tech is not staring at a gateway screen.		 SparkNotify pushes the same alarm event to Teams, Slack, WhatsApp and Telegram the moment it fires, so it reaches whichever tool the person actually has open.
Escalation means someone has to notice and forward
The shift lead manually texting the next person up when nobody acks in time.		 Escalation chains are configured in the module: if an alarm goes unacknowledged, it steps up to the next channel or contact automatically, including a Twilio voice call as the last resort.
SMS-only notification misses everyone off their desk phone plan
Sites that outgrew a single SMS gateway integration years ago.		 Twilio SMS and voice are one channel among several, not the only option, so a plant is not locked into whichever carrier the original integration happened to use.
Every new channel is a custom scripting project
The integrator who has hand-built a webhook for Slack and another for Teams.		 Each channel is a configured connector in the module, not a bespoke script per channel maintained separately and differently.

SparkNotify is in early access. Downloads are release pending; the fastest way to get on a build is to contact us directly. Early sites shape the roadmap.

For your engineers

SparkNotify is a notification module for Ignition 8.1 and 8.3 that fans out alarm pipeline events to Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram and Twilio SMS/voice from one configuration, with configurable escalation chains and acknowledgment tracking across channels. Early access; delivery and failover documentation ships with the first release build.
