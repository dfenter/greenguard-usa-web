Situations This Solves

Each row is an alarm that Ignition can raise but cannot get in front of the person who needs to see it.

The situationWhat changes
Alarms only reach people watching an Ignition client
Any plant where the on-call tech is not staring at a gateway screen.		 SparkNotify pushes the same alarm event to Teams, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram and PagerDuty the moment it fires. It reaches whichever tool the person actually has open.
Escalation means someone has to notice and forward
The shift lead manually texting the next person up when nobody acks in time.		 Escalation chains are configured in the module. If an alarm goes unacknowledged, it steps up to the next channel or contact automatically, with a Twilio voice call as the last resort.
SMS-only notification misses everyone off their desk phone plan
Sites that outgrew a single SMS gateway integration years ago.		 Twilio SMS and voice are one channel among several, not the only option. A plant is not locked into whichever carrier the original integration happened to use.
Chat and on-call tools need more than a text dump
Teams that run incidents in Discord, or page through PagerDuty rotations.		 Discord and PagerDuty are fully configurable. Discord posts rich embeds with username, avatar and thread overrides, and pings a role only on High and Critical. PagerDuty sends a full Events v2 payload with region, severity mapping and minimum-priority filtering, mirrors acknowledgements, and resolves the incident when the alarm clears.
Every new channel is a custom scripting project
The integrator who has hand-built a webhook for Slack and another for Teams.		 Each channel is a configured connector in the module, not a bespoke script maintained separately per channel.
