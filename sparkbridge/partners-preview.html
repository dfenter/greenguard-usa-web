what the first five prove

Measured, Not Claimed

A founding partner's estate becomes one of the production references this site currently lacks. We publish what was measured, at the size it was measured, with the limits printed under the drawing.

Published per founding estateDetail
ShapeWhich reference drawing it matches, site count, Host and Provider arrangement, modules installed
Throughput and bufferSustained tag changes per second at Host, store-and-forward coverage per site in hours, measured on the estate
Failure behaviorWhat happened on the first real outage: replay order, timestamp preservation, standby takeover time
Upgrade in placeEach SparkBridge release applied to the live estate, with the time it took and anything that had to be redone
Honest limitsWhat the estate does not do yet, in the same table as what it does

Questions Partners Ask First

Does the customer buy from us or from you?From you, on a registered deal. We do not quote the end customer directly while the registration stands.
What if the customer already runs a broker?Edge connects to any conformant MQTT broker. Provider is a managed broker and policy layer you can add or leave out. The review states which.
What does support cost the customer?Optional, 20% of list per year. Nothing stops running if it lapses.
Do NFR keys cover every module?Yes, every module, for the partner demo estate. Renewed yearly for Architecture Partners, never expiring for Founding Partners.
Can the case study be anonymized?Yes, at the partner's or the customer's request, at any time before or after publication.
