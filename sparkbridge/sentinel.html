A clear score, and the evidence behind it.

Sentinel connects to your plant data network, observes it, and returns a scored report of what is wrong and how serious it is. Each finding comes with the proof behind it and a specific fix, so it is an action list rather than a lecture.

It works on any plant network of this type, not only ones we built, and it reads without changing anything. Running it does not require a maintenance window.

The findings are the ones that matter commercially: whether an outsider could read your production data, whether an account that should only read can actually send commands, and whether credentials are shared in ways that cannot be revoked.
