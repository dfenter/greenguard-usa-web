SparkInject streams your plant data straight into your own Snowflake account. You own the account, the storage and the bill, and we are never in the middle of it.

Each reading is tracked individually, so a restart or a network drop cannot cause a gap or a duplicate row. Analysts stop asking whether the numbers can be trusted, and stop reconciling them by hand.

Readings keep the time the plant recorded them, so analysis of production timing, cycle times and downtime reflects what actually happened on the floor rather than when the network caught up.

8,375readings recovered after a four hour outage

During a four hour disconnection the plant kept producing and the data kept accumulating locally. When the connection returned, 8,375 readings were delivered into Snowflake, each on the time it was originally recorded, with no duplicates and nothing missing.

Outages become a delay in reporting rather than a permanent hole in the dataset.

SparkInject fits organisations that have standardised on Snowflake and want production data in it continuously, with predictable cost and without another vendor sitting in the data path. The same approach extends to AWS if your warehouse lives there instead.
