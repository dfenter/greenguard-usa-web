SparkCalc

The numbers people actually report on

Nobody reports raw sensor readings. They report efficiency, throughput, uptime and cost per unit. Those numbers are usually calculated in a spreadsheet, a dashboard and a PLC, each giving a slightly different answer.

Every derived value carries the time of its oldest input, and goes bad when any input does.

One number, calculated once, used everywhere.

SparkCalc computes derived values centrally and publishes them back as first-class plant data. Every dashboard, report and analyst then reads the same number, because there is only one.

Each value declares what it was calculated from, so when someone asks where a figure came from the answer is in the system rather than in someone's memory.

It can also recalculate over history. Define a new metric today and get it for the last six months, computed the same way, rather than waiting half a year to start trending it.

4,201historical values recomputed and independently checked

We recomputed nearly twelve hours of real plant history and checked every one of the 4,201 resulting values against an independent implementation. All 4,201 agreed on the value, the timestamp and the quality.

That window contained 17 real equipment dropouts. The derived series went bad across every one of them and never held a stale number, which is the behaviour that decides whether a reported figure can be trusted.

SparkCalc suits teams whose KPIs are recalculated in several places and do not agree, or who need a defensible answer to how a reported number was produced.
