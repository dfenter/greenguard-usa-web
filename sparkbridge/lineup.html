Why one lineup

They share a spine, and they share a discipline

These are separate products with separate licences. What makes them a lineup is that each one reuses the parts of SparkBridge that were expensive to get right, and each one is held to the same three rules.

Rule one

A value keeps its own time

Every product carries the timestamp the plant put on the reading. Nothing is restamped on arrival, so a backfill draws the real curve instead of a wall of values that all landed at once.

Rule two

An outage is not data loss

The store-and-forward buffer that SparkBridge uses to survive a broken link is the same one the historian and the injector spill to. When the far end comes back, the backlog replays in order.

Rule three

Deny by default

The broker authorizer judges every identity, including ours. Sentinel found a wildcard in our own estate that quietly contained the command topics. We fixed it and published the finding.

What shipping means here

The states on the drawing are the real ones

In service

  • Installed on production gateways.
  • Full suite green on every release, with the numbers published.
  • Signed release, checksums, upgrade notes.
  • Certified where a certification exists.

Commissioning

  • Feature complete and proven on live plant data.
  • Every claim on its page was measured, not estimated.
  • Owes a soak, a licence gate and a signed release.

On the bench

  • The hard part is built and proven against a reference implementation.
  • Not yet run against a customer's estate or hardware.
  • Talk to us if you have the hardware. Early sites shape what ships.

Held

  • Work is stopped on purpose, and the reason is written down.
  • Held beats shipping something we would not run ourselves.
