SparkADS

Talk to TwinCAT directly over native ADS/AMS

Beckhoff controllers are common on the floor, and no commercial ADS/AMS driver exists for Ignition. The usual workaround is a custom OPC UA bridge on the PLC side, one more thing to configure and maintain. SparkADS speaks ADS/AMS natively from the gateway and browses TwinCAT symbols directly.

For your engineers

SparkADS is a native Beckhoff ADS/AMS driver module for Ignition 8.1 and 8.3. It opens an AMS route to the target runtime, browses the symbol table live including structured types and arrays, and reads and writes over ADS with no OPC UA server in between. A diagnostics folder reports AMS port and route status.
