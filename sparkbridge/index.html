why

Send it when it changes, not on a timer.

Most of the time the answer is "nothing changed." Poll every site for every tag on a timer and the traffic grows with every tag you add and every dashboard you open, not with what the plant actually did. We turn it around: a site sends a value once when it changes, and any number of tools can pick it up.

Polling

Cost grows with every tag and every dashboard

A central server asks every site for every tag, over and over. Adding a dashboard or a historian adds load at the plant. One real server carrying 40 sites sat at 50 to 75% CPU, used 14 GB of memory, and couldn't ride out a network outage.

Sending on change

Cost grows only with what the plant does

A site sends a value when it changes, stamped with the time it was read, and describes its own tags once when it connects. Anything that wants the data subscribes. Adding the next system becomes a subscription, not a project.

business case

A Fraction of What You Pay Now

Licensing that counts tags, devices or connected clients charges you more every time the plant gets better instrumented. This has no such dial.

$40,300 a 40-site estate at list, once, before volume discount

That's 39 site gateways at $995 plus one central gateway at $1,495. Bought once and kept, no per-tag, per-device or per-connected-client charge on top, and nothing stops working if you stop paying for support.

What you pay for once

  • A license per gateway, bought once and kept. Not a subscription.
  • No charge per tag, however many the site grows to.
  • No charge per device, so instrumenting more of the plant is free.
  • No connected-client tier on the broker.
  • Support at 20% per year, optional. The software runs without it.
  • Development and testing, free, with no time limit.

What you stop paying for

  • Polling load that grows with every dashboard somebody adds.
  • Translator boxes between older equipment and your data, with their own licenses and patching.
  • A historian licensed and administered at every site.
  • A broker licensed by connected-client count.
  • An integration project every time another system wants the same data.
evidence

Measured, Examined, Locked Down

Every number below comes from the test suite that ships with the product, measured on a quiet six-core Linux box. That's modest hardware. Bigger hardware goes further.

Zero failures on the industry conformance suite. The independent test kit for this class of software ran ten times with zero failures. It's scripted and repeatable on your own hardware.

820 automated tests stand behind it, including live gateway integration, chaos and outage suites, and load and endurance scenarios you can run yourself.

A dropped link never loses data. Values are held safely and delivered in order once the link returns, with the timestamp they were recorded, not the timestamp they arrived.

Security is the default, not a hardening exercise. A site credential is bound to its own site, can't send commands, and can't write another site's data. Nothing gets access it wasn't explicitly granted, including our own software.
