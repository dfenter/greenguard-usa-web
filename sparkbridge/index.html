the core modules

Three Roles. One Distributed Data Architecture.

Edge moves plant data reliably. Provider carries it securely. Host turns many sites into one system. The three are separate licenses, so sites and consumers scale independently.

SparkBridge Edge

Move the data.

Publishes your Ignition tags on change, holds them on disk through an outage, and replays them in order with the original timestamps.

SparkBridge Provider

Carry the data.

Managed broker and policy layer. SparkBridge's Sparkplug-aware broker for routing, identity and policy, or connect Edge and Host through compatible broker infrastructure you already operate. $1,995 per gateway, priced separately.

SparkBridge Host

Use the data.

Subscribes every site into one namespace, learns each site's model from its birth certificate, and hosts the optional modules. Publish once at the site, add consumers at Host when you need them.
the operating idea

Publish Once. Add Consumers When You Need Them.

Each site publishes its tags one time, on change, to the broker. Every consumer of that data subscribes at the Host. Adding a historian, a warehouse feed, an OPC-UA face or an auditor changes nothing at the site: no new connection, no new polling load, no visit. Sites and consumers scale on separate lines. The plant does not need to know who will use its data next year.

At each site, once SparkBridge Edge Publishes on change. Holds through outages. Replays in order.

Consumer count does not appear on the site's bill of materials, and site count does not appear on the Host's. Forty sites with one consumer and forty sites with six consumers install the same thing at the site.

why

Six Reasons

Survive outages

Edge buffers to disk when the link drops and replays in order when it returns. Nothing is lost.

Preserve source timestamps

Every value keeps the time your equipment gave it, through an outage and back. Trends stay true.

Scale horizontally

Consumers share one stream and standbys take over in 244 ms. Add a site or a server without a ceiling.

Eliminate polling

A site sends a value once, when it changes. Nothing asks the plant for every tag on a timer.

One namespace

Every site publishes into one tree. Every tool subscribes to the same names.

No per-tag pricing

One license per gateway. Tags, devices and connected clients are never counted.
