Plant data infrastructure

Plant data you can trust.

  • Cut the cost of moving plant data, since you pay per gateway, not per tag or per dashboard.
  • Remove the risk of a bad number, since every reading keeps the time it actually happened and survives a dropped link.

Most plants can get data moving. The hard part is trusting it: knowing a number is current, knowing an outage did not leave a hole, and knowing what it cost to find out. SparkBridge connects your equipment, moves its data reliably, keeps its history, and opens it to the systems you already own.

why

Send it when it changes, not on a timer.

Most of the time the answer is "nothing changed". Asking every site for every tag on a timer grows that traffic with every tag you add and every dashboard you open, not with what the plant actually did. SparkBridge turns it around: a site sends a value once when it changes, and any number of tools can pick it up.

Polling

Cost grows with every tag and every dashboard

A central server asks every site for every tag, over and over. Adding a dashboard or a historian adds load at the plant. One real server carrying 40 sites sat at 50 to 75% CPU, used 14 GB of memory, and could not ride out a network outage.

Sending on change

Cost grows only with what the plant does

A site sends a value when it changes, stamped with the time it was read, and describes its own tags once when it connects. Anything that wants the data subscribes. Adding the next system becomes a subscription rather than a project.

modules

Seven Modules, Central Only

These seven modules only run on a SparkBridge Central gateway. Each is $495 (SparkVault $995), or all seven in the $3,995 central package. SparkLink is included with Edge, at no extra cost.

Passage

Opens your live plant data to the historians, analytics and test tools you already own. No custom connector per system.

SparkCalc

Rates, totals and efficiencies calculated once, in one place, used everywhere.

SparkID

Controls who can read your data and who can change it, plus safe credential rotation. A read permission never includes control.

SparkLink included with Edge

Keeps a remote site on a cellular or satellite link inside its data budget, measured, not estimated.

Sentinel

A scored security and health assessment of your plant data network, with the evidence and the fix for each finding.

FleetOps

One view of every gateway you run: versions, drift, certificates, backups. Fix once, apply everywhere.

Two more modules run on Central: SparkVault keeps history on the plant's own clock, and SparkInject delivers production data into your warehouse, once. See all seven with pricing.

business case

A Fraction of What You Pay Now

Licensing that counts tags, devices or connected clients charges you more every time the plant gets better instrumented. This does not have that dial.

$40,800 a 40-site estate at list, once, before volume discount

That is 39 site gateways at $995 plus one central gateway at $1,995. Bought once and kept, with no per-tag, per-device or per-connected-client charge on top of it, and nothing stops working if you stop paying for support.

What you pay for once

  • A license per gateway, bought once and kept. Not a subscription.
  • No charge per tag, however many the site grows to.
  • No charge per device, so instrumenting more of the plant is free.
  • No connected-client tier on the broker.
  • Support at 20% per year, optional. The software runs without it.
  • Development and testing, free, with no time limit.

What you stop paying for

  • Polling load that grows with every dashboard somebody adds.
  • Translator boxes between older equipment and your data, with their own licenses and patching.
  • A historian licensed and administered at every site.
  • A broker licensed by connected-client count.
  • An integration project every time another system wants the same data.
evidence

Measured, Examined, Locked Down

Every number below comes from the test suite that ships with the product, measured on a quiet six-core Linux box. It is modest hardware, and bigger hardware goes further.

Zero failures on the industry conformance suite. The independent test kit for this class of software ran ten times with zero failures. It is scripted and repeatable on your own hardware.

820 automated tests stand behind it, including live gateway integration, chaos and outage suites, and load and endurance scenarios you can run yourself.

A dropped link never loses data. Values are held safely and delivered in order once the link returns, with the timestamp they were recorded, not the timestamp they arrived.

Security is the default, not a hardening exercise. A site credential is bound to its own site, cannot send commands, and cannot write another site's data. Nothing gets access it was not explicitly granted, including our own software.
