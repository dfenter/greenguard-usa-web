You cannot control a cost you have never measured.

SparkLink measures what every tag and every site actually sends, so the monthly bill becomes a list you can act on instead of a surprise. In our own testing a single misconfigured tag was consuming half a site's entire budget.

Then it holds each site inside a budget you set, easing back on the readings that can tolerate it as the month runs on, and reporting exactly what that cost you in detail.

You mark the readings that must never be touched: anything feeding an alarm, an interlock or a regulatory report. Those are protected in the sending path itself, so no budget rule and no future change can quietly degrade them.
