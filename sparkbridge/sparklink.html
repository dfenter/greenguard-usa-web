SparkLink

Stop guessing what your remote sites cost

Remote sites on cellular or satellite links are billed by the megabyte, and almost nobody can say which tag is spending the money. The bill arrives, someone widens a few thresholds by hand, and everyone hopes.

You cannot control a cost you have never measured.

SparkLink measures what every tag and every site actually sends, so the monthly bill becomes a list you can act on instead of a surprise. In our own testing a single misconfigured tag was consuming half a site's entire budget.

Then it holds each site inside a budget you set, easing back on the readings that can tolerate it as the month runs on, and reporting exactly what that cost you in detail.

You mark the readings that must never be touched: anything feeding an alarm, an interlock or a regulatory report. Those are protected in the sending path itself, so no budget rule and no future change can quietly degrade them.

In a three day capture from a real edge, one vibration tag with no change threshold set was consuming over half the site's entire data allowance, at 47 times the next largest consumer. It was identified from the report alone, with no prior knowledge of which tag to suspect.

Under an aggressively tight budget, protected tags kept publishing at full fidelity through every test: 900 readings, 900 delivered, nothing degraded.

If you run sites on metered links and cannot name your most expensive tag, the measurement alone usually pays for this before any budget rule is switched on.
