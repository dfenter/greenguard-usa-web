HVAC and process data live in two different systems
Any plant where energy or environmental conditions affect the process but sit in a separate BAS.		 SparkBACnet browses BACnet/IP devices directly into Ignition. Supply air temperature or chiller status sits in the same tag tree as the process it affects.
A BACnet gateway box translates to Modbus for Ignition
Sites where BACnet reaches Ignition only through a protocol-translating box.		 The driver speaks BACnet/IP natively from the gateway and browses each device's object list live. The translator box and its license come out of the path.
Nobody can browse a BACnet device without vendor software
The integrator without a copy of the BAS vendor's configuration tool.		 Object lists are browsed directly over the standard protocol, the same way any BACnet-compliant device exposes them. No vendor-specific tool required.
Energy monitoring is a separate project from process monitoring
Anyone trying to correlate energy use with production without exporting from two systems.		 Building equipment and process equipment sit in the same tag tree. Correlating energy against production is a query, not a two-system export and join.

For your engineers

SparkBACnet is a native BACnet/IP driver module for Ignition 8.1 and 8.3. It discovers and browses BACnet/IP devices directly, reading object lists and present values, verified against the reference bacnet-stack implementation. A diagnostics folder reports per-device online status.
