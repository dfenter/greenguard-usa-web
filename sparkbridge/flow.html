Keep what works on the floor. Unlock everything above it.

Nothing on the floor changes. Your equipment, your broker, your Ignition gateways all keep running exactly as they do today. SparkFlow sits above them and turns what they already produce into a company-wide data stream, one that any system can tap without asking the plant for anything.

The ceiling it removes is tag count. In a conventional SCADA, every point you want to see has to become a tag, and at hundreds of thousands of points that model runs out of road. SparkFlow keeps a live, quality-stamped value for every point in the plant with no tag behind it. Your screens show what operators watch; everything else stays instantly available for history, reports and analytics. Millions of points stop being a licensing problem or a server-sizing problem. They are just there.

And the stream remembers. A new dashboard can open with the last known value of everything the moment it loads. An analyst can replay yesterday's upset minute by minute. A system that was offline for an hour catches up by itself, in order, missing nothing. When something needs to change on the floor, commands travel back down the same path, locked so that only the systems you have authorized can ever issue one.

We do not estimate reliability, we measure it. In the release benchmark, 7.25 million messages crossed a real network at 48,000 per second and every single one arrived, in order, exactly once. In a separate test we physically cut a remote site's connection for a full minute while it kept producing: every reading was held at the site and every reading reached the center when the link came back. 447 sent, 447 delivered, zero lost.

That is the standard the whole suite is built to: if your equipment said it, the number that reaches your people is the same number, with the same timestamp, no matter what the network did in between.
