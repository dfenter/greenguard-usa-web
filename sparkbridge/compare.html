why sparkbridge

Next Generation MQTT, Built to Scale

Six capabilities designed to take MQTT further with greater efficiency, scalability, and future-ready architecture.

Shared-subscription scale-out. Four consumers share one broker stream and deliver about 20x the ingest of one. We have found no competing Ignition module that removes the single-subscriber ceiling.

Published, measured numbers. Load and endurance scenarios you can run yourself, with the hardware stated. No competing module publishes comparable figures.

Change settings without a restart. Configuration hot-reloads in about five seconds. No scheduled outage to add a site or change a deadband.

Metadata folding. Units, ranges and descriptions ride the birth certificate instead of the data stream, taking roughly a third of points out of steady-state traffic.

Per-site scoping as standard. An edge credential is locked to its own site, cannot issue commands, and cannot write another site's data. Proven live against the broker, with the file-RBAC equivalent generated for estates on external HiveMQ.

No client-count tiers. The broker is not licensed by how many clients connect, so there is no 50 or 250 ceiling to design around.

Ingest and end to end are different ceilings, roughly 3x apart. The ingest row stops after decode and validate; the end-to-end row continues through projection to a value visible on a tag. Only the second describes what an operator sees, and it is the one used wherever this site compares anything.
