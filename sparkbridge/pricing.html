The drivers

One Protocol, One Price

Bought individually. A site connecting one kind of equipment needs that driver and usually not the others.

Per gateway
TI 505 · CTI 2500$495
IEC 60870-5-104$495
DNP3$495
SparkSNMP$1,495

A driver removes the translator box sitting between your equipment and your data, along with its license, its patching and its support contract. That alone is usually where it pays for itself.

SparkSNMP costs more because it replaces a licensed third-party driver and a manual setup process, not a translator box, and one license covers both current Ignition versions.

Drivers do not require SparkBridge and are not part of either package. If all you want is to read older equipment into Ignition, buy the driver alone.

Common questions

Before You Ask

What happens if support lapses?

The software keeps running exactly as it did before. You stop receiving new versions and support until you renew.

Can a license move between gateways?

Yes. A license follows the role, not the hardware, so you can move it when you replace or rebuild a server.

Can I upgrade to the package later?

Yes. Buy modules as you need them and move to the package whenever you like. You pay only the difference.

Is development really free?

Yes, with no time limit and nothing held back. You only buy a license once a gateway goes into production.
