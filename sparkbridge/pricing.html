Pricing

Priced per gateway, kept for good

Free for development and testing, so you can prove it works before anyone signs anything. When you go live you buy one licence per gateway and keep it. There is no charge per tag, per device or per connected client.

Buy the one thing you need, or the package it sits in.

Every licence is bought once per gateway and kept. Nothing stops working if you stop paying for support, and nothing is metered.

Two packages cover the usual shapes. The site gateway package is SparkBridge Edge plus every module that runs at a site. The central server suite is SparkBridge Central plus every module that runs centrally. If you want less than that, buy SparkBridge on its own and add single modules at $495 each.

The protocol drivers sit outside both packages. They are bought on their own, one per protocol, because a utility connecting an RTU usually needs exactly one of them.

Prices below are list, before volume discount. Bring the quote you have from anyone else and we will put the two side by side for your real site count.

Common questions

Before You Ask

What happens if support lapses?

The software keeps running exactly as it did. You stop receiving new versions and support until you renew.

Can a licence move between gateways?

Yes. A licence follows the gateway role, so you can move it when you replace or rebuild the hardware.

Can I upgrade to a package later?

Yes. Buy modules as you need them and move to a package whenever you like, paying only the difference.

Is development really free?

Yes, with no time limit and no feature gate. You only buy a licence when a gateway goes into production.

Every price here is list, per gateway, before volume discount. For an estate of any size the number that matters is your site count, so tell us that and we will do the arithmetic with you rather than making you do it from a table.
