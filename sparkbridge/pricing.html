Pricing

Priced per gateway, kept for good

Free for development and testing, so you can prove it works before anyone signs anything. When you go live you buy one license per gateway and keep it. There is no charge per tag, per device or per connected client.

Buy the one thing you need, or the package it sits in.

Every license is bought once per gateway and kept. Nothing stops working if you stop paying for support, and nothing is metered.

A site gateway is SparkBridge Edge, $995, standalone, with SparkLink included; there is no site package because nothing else installs at the edge. A central gateway is SparkBridge Central, $1,995, standalone. Every central module is bought separately at $495 (SparkVault $995), or all seven come together in the $3,995 central package: Passage, SparkCalc, SparkID, Sentinel, FleetOps, SparkVault and SparkInject, which a la carte is $5,960. Identity, rotation, namespace exposure and fleet control are the things broker vendors sell as an enterprise edition priced per connected client; here each is one module, bought once.

SparkFlow, SparkSNMP and the protocol drivers are always billed separately and are never part of the package. The drivers are bought one per protocol, because a utility connecting an RTU usually needs exactly one of them.

Prices below are list, before volume discount. Bring the quote you have from anyone else and we will put the two side by side for your real site count, starting from the feature-by-feature comparison.

The drivers

One Protocol, One Price

Bought individually, because a site connecting a particular kind of equipment needs that driver and usually not the others.

Per gateway
TI 505 · CTI 2500$495
IEC 60870-5-104$495
DNP3$495

A driver removes the translator hardware between your equipment and your data, along with its license, its patching and its support contract. That is usually where it pays for itself, before anything is said about fidelity.

SparkSNMP is priced above the other drivers because it replaces a licensed third party driver and a MIB workflow, not a translator box, and one license covers both the 8.1 and 8.3 builds.

Drivers do not require SparkBridge and are not part of either package. If all you want is to read older equipment into Ignition, buy the driver alone.

Common questions

Before You Ask

What happens if support lapses?

The software keeps running exactly as it did. You stop receiving new versions and support until you renew.

Can a license move between gateways?

Yes. A license follows the gateway role, so you can move it when you replace or rebuild the hardware.

Can I upgrade to a package later?

Yes. Buy modules as you need them and move to a package whenever you like, paying only the difference.

Is development really free?

Yes, with no time limit and no feature gate. You only buy a license when a gateway goes into production.

Every price here is list, per gateway, before volume discount. For an estate of any size the number that matters is your site count, so tell us that and we will do the arithmetic with you rather than making you do it from a table.
