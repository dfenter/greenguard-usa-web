Common Questions

What happens if we stop paying support?

The software keeps running. A perpetual license does not expire and does not phone home. You stop receiving updates and you lose the support channel, and you can resume later without repurchasing the license.

Is the development tier crippled?

No. It is the same build with the same capabilities and the same limits. The only restriction is that it is not licensed for production use. A gate that hides the thing you need to evaluate would waste your time and ours.

Do we need a license for a redundant or backup gateway?

No. A backup gateway that is not carrying production traffic is not a second production gateway. Redundancy is a reliability requirement, not a licensing event.

What about the broker?

Included. The Provider module runs an embedded broker, or manages a HiveMQ CE companion, and neither is licensed by connected-client count. There is no client-count tier to size around.

Who supports it?

The engineers who built it, directly. No ticket queue and no first-line script: the person answering is the person who wrote the code. Support and updates are included with volume agreements and available at 20%/yr on any gateway license.

Can we get the source?

Talk to us. Source or escrow arrangements are part of the enterprise conversation rather than a published SKU.
