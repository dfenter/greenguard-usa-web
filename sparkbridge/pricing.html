Pricing

Simple pricing

Free to use for development and testing, so you can prove it works before anyone signs anything. When you go live you buy a license once per gateway and keep it.

Buy the one thing you need, or the whole line.

Every licence is bought once per gateway and kept. There is no charge per tag, per device or per connected client, and nothing stops working if you stop paying for support.

The line splits into two families because they solve different problems for different people. The data products share one foundation and are priced to be added to it. The protocol drivers are bought on their own, one per protocol, because a utility connecting an RTU usually needs exactly one of them.

Prices below are list, before volume discount. Bring the quote you have from anyone else and we will put the two side by side for your real site count.

The drivers

One protocol, one price

Bought individually, because a site connecting a particular kind of equipment needs that driver and usually not the others.

Per gateway
TI 505 · CTI 2500$995
IEC 60870-5-104$995
DNP3$995

A driver removes the translator hardware between your equipment and your data, along with its licence, its patching and its support contract. That is usually where it pays for itself, before anything is said about fidelity.

Drivers do not require SparkBridge. If all you want is to read older equipment into Ignition, buy the driver alone.
