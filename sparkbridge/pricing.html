How licensing works

What happens if support lapses?

The software keeps running exactly as it did before. You stop receiving new versions and support until you renew.

Can I add modules later?

Yes. Buy each module as your site needs it, on its own license, on a Central gateway you already own.

Is development really free?

Yes. Every paid module runs on Ignition's two-hour module trial until a key file names it; Reset Trial restarts the clock, and a key lifts the pause within thirty seconds with no restart. SparkLink, SparkCalc and SparkID never pause. You buy a license once a gateway goes into production.
