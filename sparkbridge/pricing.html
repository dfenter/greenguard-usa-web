A driver removes the translator box sitting between your equipment and your data, along with its license, its patching and its support contract. That alone is usually where it pays for itself.

SparkLogix connects Allen-Bradley Logix controllers directly and browses tags live, faster and safer than polling through a translator.

We price SparkSNMP higher because it replaces a licensed third-party driver and a manual setup process, not a translator box. One license covers both current Ignition versions.

SparkADS, SparkS7, Spark61850 and SparkBACnet are in early access: native ADS/AMS, symbolic S7, IEC 61850 MMS and BACnet/IP drivers, built where there is no commercial incumbent or where Inductive Automation removed symbolic addressing. Downloads are release pending; contact us to get on a build.

Every driver carries its own license and works with or without SparkBridge. If all you want is to read older or Allen-Bradley equipment into Ignition, buy the driver alone.

Common questions

Common Questions

What happens if support lapses?

The software keeps running exactly as it did before. You stop receiving new versions and support until you renew.

Can a license move between gateways?

Yes. A license follows the role, not the hardware, so you can move it when you replace or rebuild a server.

Can I add modules later?

Yes. Buy each module as your site needs it, on its own license.

Is development really free?

Yes, with no time limit and nothing held back. You only buy a license once a gateway goes into production.
