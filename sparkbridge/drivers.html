Device drivers

Drivers are not part of SparkBridge. They are sold on their own.

No device driver is included with any SparkBridge license or package, not Edge, not Central, not the $3,995 Central package. Every driver on this page is its own per-gateway perpetual license: buy the one your site needs. Each works with or without SparkBridge installed on the gateway, and each is free to use in development with no time limit.

What IS included with SparkBridge

So there is no ambiguity about where the line sits.

SparkBridge Edge ($995) includes SparkLink at no extra cost. Any SparkBridge license (Edge or Central) also includes SparkCalc, Passage and SparkID at no extra cost. The $3,995 Central package adds Passage, SparkCalc, SparkID, Sentinel, FleetOps, SparkVault and SparkInject on top of SparkBridge Central, at a savings over buying the seven separately.

No device driver is in that list, and no device driver is ever added to it. SparkLogix, SparkSNMP, SparkADS, SparkS7, Spark61850, SparkBACnet, IEC 60870-5-104 and DNP3 are always bought on their own, whether or not the site also runs SparkBridge.
