What comes with a SparkBridge license

Everything a SparkBridge license or package includes, in one place.

SparkBridge Edge ($995) includes SparkLink at no extra cost. Any SparkBridge license (Edge or Central) also includes SparkCalc, Passage and SparkID at no extra cost. The $3,995 Central package adds Passage, SparkCalc, SparkID, Sentinel, FleetOps, SparkVault and SparkInject on top of SparkBridge Central, at a savings over buying the seven on their own.

Device drivers are their own product line, each with its own per-gateway price, listed below. SparkLogix, SparkSNMP, SparkADS, SparkS7, Spark61850, SparkBACnet, IEC 60870-5-104 and DNP3 each run on any gateway, with or without a SparkBridge license.
