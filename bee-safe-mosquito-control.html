Why Most Approaches Harm Bees

The problem with conventional mosquito spraying

Every conventional mosquito spray product uses broad-spectrum insecticides. When applied to your yard, these compounds land on the same flowering plants that bees and butterflies feed from. There is no application technique that targets mosquitoes while sparing pollinators, the mechanism of action affects all insects it contacts.

CO₂ trapping is different at the fundamental level. The trap attracts mosquitoes using CO₂ and human scent compounds, signals that blood-seeking insects use to locate hosts. Bees navigate using floral volatiles and visual cues that have nothing to do with CO₂. They are not attracted to the trap and are not affected by its operation. Read our pollinator garden guide.

What makes GreenGuard USA different

Our protection technology releases nothing into the environment that is not already in the atmosphere. CO₂ is present naturally at 0.04% of the air around you. The human scent attractants dissipate in open air. There is no residue on your garden plants, no contamination of nectar or pollen, and no waiting period before bees can safely forage.
