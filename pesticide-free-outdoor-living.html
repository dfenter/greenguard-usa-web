What Pesticide-Free Really Means

No residue. No drift. No waiting.

Pesticide-free is a term that gets used loosely in the outdoor protection industry. For GreenGuard USA, it means exactly what it says: no pesticide compound is applied to your property at any stage of our service.

Our CO₂ protection technology works through physical capture, not pesticide application. The trap releases CO₂ and human scent attractants, draws mosquitoes in, and contains them. Nothing is sprayed, fogged, or applied to vegetation, soil, furniture, or any outdoor surface. Your children can play in the yard during and after service. Your pets can be outside. Your food garden is untouched.

The Full Pesticide-Free Experience

Every part of our service is clean

Many companies market pesticide reduction while still applying pesticides as part of their service. GreenGuard USA's outdoor protection program is pesticide-free from start to finish.

Our delivery fleet is all-electric. Our CO₂ tanks contain only food-grade carbon dioxide. Our attractants are organic compounds that dissipate in open air. Every component of the service, from our vehicles to the catch net inside the trap, involves zero pesticide exposure for you, your family, or your property.
