Why Eco-Friendly Matters

The environmental cost of conventional mosquito spraying

Traditional mosquito spray programs apply synthetic pyrethroids across your entire yard on a schedule. These compounds are indiscriminate, killing mosquitoes and bees, beneficial soil insects, and aquatic organisms in any drainage features on or near your property.

Studies have documented bee population reductions of up to 70% following residential pyrethroid applications. For families who have deliberately planted native gardens, maintained pollinator habitats, or simply value a functioning backyard ecosystem, this is not a tolerable tradeoff.
