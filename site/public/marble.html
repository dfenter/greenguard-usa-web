MARBLE MANIA

Roll your marble from the start pad to the glowing goal. Stay on the track. Fall off and you respawn, losing precious seconds. Beat all 18 courses before the clock runs out. New: yellow springs bounce you up, orange cannons blast you across gaps, purple chutes send you flying downhill.
