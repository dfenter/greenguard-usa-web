# Driftwood Cove licenses

Driftwood Cove ships original procedural art and original procedural audio
generated for this title. No external art pack files are shipped under this
directory, and nothing is hotlinked from another game directory.

- Phaser 3 is loaded from `/play/_shared/phaser.min.js` under the MIT license.
- GGKit is loaded from `/play/_shared/ggkit.js` and is GreenGuard studio original work.
- Every item, board frame, card, ring, particle, diorama and icon is drawn in
  code into canvas textures inside `game.js`. The palette, silhouettes, symbols
  and materials are original title work.
- `assets/*.mp3` are original synthesised cues and loops (marimba, glass, wood
  and surf models) rendered offline for this title, mono mp3 only, never ogg.
- `icon.png`, `icon512.png` and `favicon.png` are original artwork drawn for
  this title.
- Art direction follows `play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md`; UI rules follow
  `play/_assets/UI_LAW.md`; the asset policy is recorded in
  `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`.
- All names, characters, notes and story text are original. No licensed or
  lookalike characters, logos, trade dress or third party text is used.
