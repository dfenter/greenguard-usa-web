# Molehunt Manor licenses

The game code, content, silhouettes, manor layout, UI, and procedural effects
are original GreenGuard USA work.

## Runtime

Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/`; their licenses are
covered by [`../_shared/LICENSES.md`](../_shared/LICENSES.md).

## Audio

`assets/music_manor.mp3`, `assets/music_tension.mp3`, and the ten `assets/sfx_*.mp3`
files are short procedural studio tones generated for this title. They contain
no third-party samples and are used through GGKit audio buses.

## Art ledger

No harvested art pack files are shipped. The title follows the party/UI art
direction in `play/_assets/ART_partyui.md`; the asset inventory and provenance
rules are recorded in `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`.

## Icons

`icon.png`, `icon512.png`, and `favicon.png` are procedural original title
marks generated for the PWA shell.
