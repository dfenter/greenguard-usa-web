# Carnival Reels
Controls: tap SPIN (or space) to play; tap a machine tab (or 1-4) to switch; -/+ (or arrow left/right) sets bet; RST resets the bank free at any time (or R); the speaker button mutes (M); drag the lower panel or use arrow up/down to scroll it.
Loop: pick one of four original machines, set a bet, spin. Every spin updates the live math panel underneath - exact or simulated RTP, house edge, volatility, the full outcome table with real frequencies, and your own observed return - plus a session bank curve, a goal ladder to beat your session high, and oddity badges for rare lines with their true odds shown.
No fail state, no real money, no currencies for sale: the bank is play money and resets free, forever. Best peak, lifetime per-machine results and badges persist locally.
