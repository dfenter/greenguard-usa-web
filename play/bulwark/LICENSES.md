# Bulwark licenses

Bulwark ships no third-party gameplay raster art. Battlefield terrain, unit silhouettes, tower silhouettes, range rings, map landmarks, particles, UI chrome, and the app icon are original procedural work in `engine.js` and `game.js`.

The strategy treatment was checked against [`play/_assets/ART_strategy.md`](../_assets/ART_strategy.md). The asset source ledger was checked at [`play/_assets/LEDGER.md`](../_assets/LEDGER.md); no harvested image or audio files were available there for this rebuild.

The small MP3 files in `assets/audio/` are original procedural tones rendered for Bulwark and are used only through GGKit audio buses. They are not copied from another title and contain no external samples.

Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/` and are covered by [`play/_shared/LICENSES.md`](../_shared/LICENSES.md).
