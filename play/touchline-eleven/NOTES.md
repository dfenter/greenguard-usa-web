# Touchline Eleven
Drag from the carrier to pass; swipe toward the top goal to shoot; tap a defender to switch and tackle.
Use arrows to move, J to pass, and K to shoot. Tap PASS or SHOOT for a quick action.
Pick five players between fixtures; wins add two squad players and trophies persist on this device.
Seven matches make a cup; a loss replays the fixture. Matches last three minutes and there are no paid gates.
