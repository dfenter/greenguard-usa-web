Controls: drag the left stick or use WASD to steer Rook; tap a road node to build/upgrade; 1–4 select turrets.
Loop: survive 25 authored waves, use the preview to prepare, choose one tech pick between waves, and collect scrap.
Turrets: gun (direct), slow field (stacking), mortar (blast), tesla (chain); tap BARRIER to redirect a split.
Lose when the core breaks; win after wave 25; tap RESTART or press R to run again. Best score is device-local.
