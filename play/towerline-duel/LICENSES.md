# Towerline Duel licenses

## Code and generated art

`game.js` and the procedural board, unit silhouettes, core marks, card icons,
particles, and PWA icons are original GreenGuard USA work generated for this
title. No external game assets are hotlinked or copied into the title.

## Shared runtime

Phaser 3 is loaded from `/play/_shared/phaser.min.js` and GGKit from
`/play/_shared/ggkit.js`. Their licensing terms are recorded in
`/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

## Audio

The five short MP3 cues in `assets/` are procedurally generated for this title
and routed through GGKit audio buses. They contain no third-party samples.

## Asset ledger

The title uses no harvested image pack. The strategy art rules and available
pack provenance are cited from `play/_assets/ART_strategy.md` and
`play/_assets/LEDGER.md` as required by the fleet asset ledger.
