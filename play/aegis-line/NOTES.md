Controls: tap a mech to target; tap glowing weak points for crits; swipe from a portrait to a mech; tap portraits 1-2-3 (breaker, sustain, burst) for a chain.
Keyboard: arrows select a lane/target; 1-3 trigger bursts; R retries or opens roster; Enter deploys.
Loop: clear 12 advancing stages, bosses every 4; unlock squadmates at milestones and skins through achievements.
Progress, best score, unlocked pilots, and skins persist locally; no network, currency, ads, or energy.
