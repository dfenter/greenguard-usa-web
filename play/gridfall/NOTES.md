# Gridfall
Touch: drag a piece from the 3-slot hand onto the 8x8 board (piece rides above your finger, ghost shows the snap + which lines would pop). Keys: arrows move, 1/2/3 pick a slot, Tab cycles, Space drops, R restarts.
Loop: place pieces, complete any full row or column to clear it; consecutive clearing placements build a STREAK multiplier, a full wipe pays +300. Run ends when nothing in the hand fits.
Modes: MARATHON (endless) and DAILY (date-seeded board + piece stream, first finish of the day is the logged score). Bests persist per mode; last 10 runs show on the game-over card.
Better than the leader: the NEXT hand is always visible, and hands are validated before dealing so you never get a dead hand.
No build step, no network, no cookies — open index.html.
