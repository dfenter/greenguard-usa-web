# Tide Harbor licenses

## Original title code and procedural art

- `game.js`, the UI, the procedural Three.js vessel silhouettes, regions, docks, landmarks, water, wake, spray, rain, storm cells, caches, crates, and wind lanes are original GreenGuard studio work.
- No third-party model, texture, image, or font asset is shipped by this title.
- The fourteen MP3 cues in `assets/` are procedural audio renders made for this title and contain no third-party recording. `wind.mp3` and `market.mp3` are looping music beds. The remaining twelve files are distinct title SFX.
- Audio inventory: `wind.mp3`, `market.mp3`, `creak.mp3`, `gulls.mp3`, `storm.mp3`, `trim.mp3`, `dock.mp3`, `buy.mp3`, `sell.mp3`, `reef.mp3`, `cache.mp3`, `boost.mp3`, `upgrade.mp3`, and `victory.mp3`.

## Shared runtime

- `ggkit.js` is the GreenGuard studio kit, original work, no third-party code.
- Three.js r160.1 is loaded from `/play/_shared/three/three.module.min.js` under its MIT license. The shared license record is `/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

## Asset ledger citation

The allowed asset inventory and provenance rules are recorded in `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md`. Tide Harbor uses no harvested pack files. This title follows the ledger rule that only curated, traced assets may ship under a title directory.
