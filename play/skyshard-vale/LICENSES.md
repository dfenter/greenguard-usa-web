# Skyshard Vale licenses

## Original title code and visuals

All game code, procedural world art, character silhouettes, enemy families,
icons, UI, and effects in this directory are original GreenGuard USA work for
Skyshard Vale. No third-party title directory is referenced or hotlinked.

## Generated audio

The MP3 cues in `assets/` are short procedural tones generated locally for
this title. They contain no sampled third-party audio and carry no external
license.

## Shared runtime

Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/`. Their license notices are
in [`../_shared/LICENSES.md`](../_shared/LICENSES.md).

## Asset ledger

No harvested art pack is shipped. The per-title asset choice follows the
procedural option in [`../_assets/LEDGER.md`](../_assets/LEDGER.md); the ledger
was reviewed for this rebuild and no pack row is claimed.
