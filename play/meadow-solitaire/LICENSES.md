# Meadow Solitaire licenses

## Procedural title art and audio

All Meadow Solitaire card faces, backs, meadow scenes, interface chrome,
particles, icons, and music/SFX stems are original procedural work created for
this title. The MP3 files under `assets/` are locally generated tone beds and
one-shot effects. No remote assets or network services are used.

## Runtime

Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/` and are covered by
[`play/_shared/LICENSES.md`](../_shared/LICENSES.md).

## Asset ledger

The title was checked against [`play/_assets/LEDGER.md`](../_assets/LEDGER.md).
No third-party pack files were copied into this title. Procedural generation is
used where an authored bitmap or audio file is required.
