# Kinetic Burst
Controls: drag across the orb board to trace a path (release or 6.5s timer fires it); tap an enemy card to target; tap anywhere when the CLASH bar sweeps into the gold zone. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, SPACE starts/commits a trace and taps clashes, 1/2/3 target, M math, T team, R restart.
Loop: every run of 3+ same-type orbs in your traced path charges the fighter of that ki type (hearts heal the team); a fighter at 100% charge strikes with a timing-tap clash for up to x2.00 damage.
Ki triangle POWER > SPEED > FOCUS > POWER (x1.50 / x1.00 / x0.67); enemies telegraph their target and damage at least one turn ahead.
Win a round by downing all three opponents; team KO retries the round instantly. 8-round ladder = the crown; 2 more fighters join the roster after rounds 2, 4 and 6 (earned by playing - nothing is sold). MATH panel shows every rate and formula.
