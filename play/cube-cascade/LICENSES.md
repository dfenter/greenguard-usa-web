# Cube Cascade licenses

## Runtime

- Phaser 3 is loaded only from `/play/_shared/phaser.min.js`, covered by `/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.
- GGKit is loaded only from `/play/_shared/ggkit.js`, covered by `/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.
- The service worker follows `/play/_shared/sw-template.js`.

## Art and audio

- Cube, column, warning, HUD, background, icon, and particle visuals are procedural original work in `game.js` and the generated PNG icons.
- The `/play/_assets/ART_arcade2d.md` bible and `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md` were used as the visual and provenance constraints.
- The audio cues and base/danger music stems in `assets/` are short procedural MP3s generated for this title. No external audio URL or file is used. The LEDGER CC0 audio packs remain available for a future curated replacement, but none are copied into this build.

No third-party title assets are referenced.
