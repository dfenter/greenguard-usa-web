# Spire Ascent
Controls: TAP anywhere = jump (hold = higher); DOUBLE-TAP in mid-air = dash — dash into a wall for a wall-kick. Desktop: Space/W/Up/Enter (hold), double-press to dash, A/D or arrows nudge direction, R restarts.
Loop: you auto-run and flip direction off the walls; climb seeded tower rows of ledges, crumblers, springs, movers, wind zones and spike bands while the lava doom line rises faster over time.
Scoring: score = climb height + combo bonus for consecutive landings on non-crumbling platforms; falling into the lava or touching spikes ends the run.
Persistence: best score in localStorage for ENDLESS and separately for DAILY SEED (mode toggled on the game-over screen); tap anywhere to restart instantly.
