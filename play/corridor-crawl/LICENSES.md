# Corridor Crawl licenses

Corridor Crawl's visible tile, monster, item, particle, and icon art is
procedurally drawn original IP in `game.js`.

The game uses the vendored Phaser 3 runtime and GGKit from `play/_shared/`.
Their license notices are in [`play/_shared/LICENSES.md`](../_shared/LICENSES.md).

The art and audio bible is [`play/_assets/ART_topdown2d.md`](../_assets/ART_topdown2d.md).
The shared asset provenance ledger is [`play/_assets/LEDGER.md`](../_assets/LEDGER.md).
The local `assets/corridor-atlas.svg` and `assets/audio/*.mp3` files are
original GreenGuard title assets generated for this game. No harvested pack
files or hotlinked media are used. All audio routes through GGKit's registered
music and SFX buses.
