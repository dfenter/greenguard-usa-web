Kart Circuit Zero
Controls: drag left/right on the left half to steer; hold DRIFT on the right and release to boost.
Keyboard: arrows/WASD steer, Space drifts, R restarts, 1–4 choose circuits, Enter restarts after a run.
Loop: race 3 laps on one of four seeded circuits; beat the medal time and your saved ghost.
Best times and best-run ghosts persist in localStorage on this device.
