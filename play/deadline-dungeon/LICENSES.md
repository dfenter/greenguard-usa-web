# Deadline Dungeon licenses

Deadline Dungeon uses original procedural pixel art generated in `game.js`:
the hero and enemy sheets, room boards, HUD chrome, pickups, gate, hazards,
and particles are not copied from another title.

The compact MP3 effects under `assets/` are original procedural tones made for
this title. They contain no third-party recordings and use the GGKit audio
buses. No OGG files are used.

Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/` and are covered by
`/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`. The art direction follows
`/play/_assets/ART_topdown2d.md`. The asset provenance rules and CC0 pack
ledger are documented in `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md`; this rebuild uses no
harvested pack files.
