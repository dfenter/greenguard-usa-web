- `game.js` contains original procedural reef, tile, fish, decor, particle, and UI art authored for GreenGuard USA. No third-party art is bundled.
- Audio in `assets/` is original procedural MP3 synthesis generated for this title, including `reef-meta.mp3`. No OGG audio is used.
- Phaser 3 is loaded from `/play/_shared/phaser.min.js`. GGKit is loaded from `/play/_shared/ggkit.js`; see `/play/_shared/LICENSES.md` for shared-runtime provenance.
- The visual system follows `/play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md` and `/play/_assets/UI_LAW.md`. Asset provenance was checked against `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md`.
