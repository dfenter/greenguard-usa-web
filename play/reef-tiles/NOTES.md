# Reef Tiles

Controls: swipe (or tap two neighbours) to swap tiles; PLAY/TANK/SHOP tabs at the bottom. Tank: tap water to drop food, drag decor to rearrange. Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space/Enter selects-swaps or feeds, G grabs decor, Tab cycles buttons, R retries, M mutes.
Loop: clear seeded match-3 levels inside a move budget to hit the tile goals and earn pearls; spend pearls in the shop on 16 decor pieces and 6 fish species.
Fish live in the tank with real behaviour (schooling, hiding in plants, floor-hugging, chasing dropped food, bubbling at vents); plants + hides + vents + fed fish raise TANK COMFORT.
Comfort 30 unlocks levels 6-10, comfort 60 unlocks levels 11-15 — so growing the aquarium is the gate, never money or timers.
Everything is play-earned and offline: no purchases, no energy, no ads; progress, pearls and the whole tank persist in localStorage.
