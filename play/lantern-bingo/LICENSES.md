# Lantern Bingo licenses

- `game.js` art is original procedural work authored for Lantern Bingo. Every
  city backdrop, card face, lantern, souvenir emblem, keeper frame, pattern
  icon, chip icon and word mark is baked into a canvas texture in code. No
  image pack, font file, CDN or remote content is shipped.
- `icon.png`, `icon512.png`, `favicon.png` are original raster art generated
  for this title.
- `assets/*.mp3` are original procedural cues and music beds synthesised for
  this title and played through the GGKit audio buses. Shipped as mono MP3
  (libmp3lame) per the mobile audio format law. No `.ogg` is shipped.
  - Music beds: `music_lantern.mp3`, `music_hall.mp3`, `music_skyfire.mp3`
  - Effects: `tap`, `daub`, `streak`, `miss`, `call`, `charge`, `chip`,
    `oneaway`, `bingo`, `rivalwin`, `souvenir`, `back`, `start`

- `ggkit.js` is GreenGuard studio kit original work. Its notice lives in
  `play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.
- Asset policy and provenance ledger: `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`. This rebuild
  ships no harvested pack files, so no pack row applies to it.
- All names, rooms, cities, souvenirs, rival names and text are original. No
