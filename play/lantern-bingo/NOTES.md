Controls: tap the glowing called number on any card; keys 1-4 daub cards; tap chips; V opens bot cards.
Loop: clear the room goal before 75 calls; daub streaks charge Auto, Double, and Peek chips.
Rooms: four rooms add cards and speed; wins unlock the next room; blackout wins earn crowns.
Free by design: infinite sparks, no gates, no purchases, no network.
