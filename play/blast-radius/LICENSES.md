# Blast Radius licenses

Blast Radius uses no harvested raster, vector, or font assets. The SVG tokens,
UI, icons, particles, music stems, and sound cues are original procedural work
for this title.

- Phaser 3 and GGKit are loaded from `/play/_shared/` and are covered by
  `play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.
- The visual direction follows `play/_assets/ART_arcade2d.md`.
- The asset inventory and provenance rules are documented in
  `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`.
- The generated MP3 cues and music stems in `assets/` are original procedural
  synthesis for this game. The SVG texture set in `assets/` is original title
  artwork. No OGG or other audio formats are shipped.
