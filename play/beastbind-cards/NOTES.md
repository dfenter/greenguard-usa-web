# Beastbind Cards
Controls: drag a card from hand to the ACTIVE or a BENCH slot (tap-select then tap-slot also works); drop an evolution onto its pre-evolution; tap ENERGY then a creature to attach; tap an attack row to strike; RETREAT then a bench slot; END TURN. Keyboard: arrows move focus, Enter activates, Esc backs out.
Loop: 20-card deck, active + 3 bench, one energy per turn, evolve on later turns, knockouts give prize markers — 3 markers wins the rank. Beat all 8 ladder decks (each with a posted archetype tell) to become champion.
Economy: every 2 wins grants a free pack (5 cards, pull rates posted on the pack screen). Max 2 copies of a card are kept; a 3rd copy becomes +1 credit, and 5 credits claim ANY missing card — the full 60-card set lands around 60 wins. Nothing is purchasable.
Persistence: rank, wins, collection, credits and deck are stored in localStorage under `beastbind.v1`.
