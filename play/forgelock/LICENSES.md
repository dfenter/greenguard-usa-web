# Forgelock - asset licenses

Rev 1, 2026-08-10. Forgelock uses Phaser 3 and GGKit from `/play/_shared/`.
Those licenses are covered by `/play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

The board, HUD, icons, particle textures, core and forge marks are procedural
original work authored for GreenGuard USA. The six short audio cues in
`assets/` are procedural original work encoded as mono MP3 for the GGKit audio
buses. No OGG files are shipped and no runtime network asset is referenced.
Nothing is copied from another title directory.

The visual direction follows `/play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md`: enamel steel,
slate and brass framing; saturated pieces with symbol and value coding; quiet
cell fields; pooled tactile fragments; and reduced-motion gating.

The `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md` was consulted for the Puzzle Pop lane and its
Kenney CC0 audio and UI pack rows. No harvested pack file is consumed because
the title's authored sound cues and procedural marks are self-contained. The
ledger remains unchanged.
