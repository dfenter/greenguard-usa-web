Controls: swipe the worker one cell to push crates; keyboard arrows/WASD also move.
Undo: tap UNDO or press Z; RESET and R restart the current level instantly.
Loop: align every colored core with its matching forge; conveyors, gates, and plates add hazards.
Progress: completed levels, stars, and unlocks persist in localStorage on this device.
Construction: all 30 boards are reverse-walked from solved states, so each has a known solution.
