# Transit Dash licenses

Transit Dash uses no third-party runtime art. All visual textures and audio
files under this title are original procedural work generated for GreenGuard
USA's Transit Dash rebuild.

The engine files are loaded from `/play/_shared/` and are covered by
`play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

The visual direction follows `/play/_assets/ART_arcade2d.md` and the asset
inventory rules in `/play/_assets/LEDGER.md`. No files from another title are
referenced or copied.
