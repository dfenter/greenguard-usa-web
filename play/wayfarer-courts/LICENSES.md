  rival silhouette, tile map, hub illustration, battle backdrop, UI mark and

## Audio (all original, synthesised from scratch)

Every clip in `assets/` was generated by an original additive and FM synthesis
script written for this title (no samples, no packs, no recordings). All files
are mono MP3 (libmp3lame) per the mobile audio format law: no `.ogg` ships.

Music loops (5): `town.mp3`, `field.mp3`, `battle.mp3`, `court.mp3`,
`celestial.mp3`.

Effects (16): `strike.mp3`, `art.mp3`, `guard.mp3`, `hurt.mp3`, `heal.mp3`,
`bond.mp3`, `ui.mp3`, `back.mp3`, `step.mp3`, `reward.mp3`, `craft.mp3`,
`rank.mp3`, `defeat.mp3`, `victory.mp3`, `quest.mp3`, `encounter.mp3`.
