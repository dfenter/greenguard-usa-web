# Orbit Hearts
Controls: tap to advance text / tap a choice / tap on the beat during date scenes. Keyboard: space or enter = advance & tap, arrows = move between buttons, M = mute, Esc = title.
Loop: pick one of three love interests on Vireo Station, play 3 chapters, move their affinity with choices and 3-beat rhythm dates, hit a mid-route branch that rewrites chapter 3, land 1 of 3 endings.
Replay: 9 endings total; the constellation map lights a star per ending found, keeps best affinity per route and recaps the choices from your last run (saved locally).
Free by design: every route, chapter and ending is unlocked from the start — no currencies, energy, or timers.
