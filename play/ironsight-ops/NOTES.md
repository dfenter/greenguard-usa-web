Controls: left pad/WASD move; right pad/arrows aim and fire; G or GADGET uses the selected gadget.
Loop: choose map/mode/loadout, clear a 2-minute warehouse or courtyard drill, then run it back.
Modes: TDM is first to 20; Control is first to 100 while holding the center zone.
Progress: rank XP and earned camo colors persist locally; there are no purchases or gates.
