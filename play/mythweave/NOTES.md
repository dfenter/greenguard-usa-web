# Mythweave
Controls: tap 5-card hand to pick 3 per turn (keys 1-5), tap a foe to target (arrows), RESOLVE/Enter to fire.
Loop: 3 chapters x 4 battles; spirits join as story rewards and each adds cards to the shared hand.
Chaining 3 cards from one spirit fires that spirit's ultimate; optional bond fights upgrade a spirit's cards.
Wipe = free retry, chapter 3 finale = win; roster, bonds and best run time persist in localStorage.
