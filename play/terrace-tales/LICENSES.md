Terrace Tales uses original procedural art and original procedural audio generated for this title. No external art pack files are shipped under this directory.

- Phaser 3.87.0 is loaded from `/play/_shared/phaser.min.js` under the MIT license.
- GGKit is loaded from `/play/_shared/ggkit.js` and is GreenGuard studio original work.
- The palette, symbols, garden props, board chrome, particles, and audio cues are original title work.
- The playable art direction follows `play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md`.
- The board palette and shape coding follow `play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md`, and the asset policy is recorded in `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`.
