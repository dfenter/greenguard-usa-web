# Backstreet Reckoning licenses

Backstreet Reckoning uses Phaser 3 and GGKit from `play/_shared/`. Their
license notices are covered by `play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

The game ships no third-party art. Fighter sheets, gang sheets, street
landmarks, particles, HUD textures, icons, and the short audio cues are
procedurally authored for this title. Audio playback is routed through the
GGKit music and SFX buses. No remote URLs, external fonts, or hotlinked game
assets are used.

The visual direction follows `play/_assets/ART_arcade2d.md`. The asset ledger
was reviewed at `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`; no ledger pack is copied into this
title.
