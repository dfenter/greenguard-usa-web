# Curbside — endless skate (portrait, canvas + vanilla JS)
Controls: tap = ollie; in the air swipe LEFT/RIGHT/UP/DOWN = Backspin Curl / Gutter Whip / Skylatch Grab / Drop Shove; swipe UP over a rail or ledge = grind, then press-and-micro-drag to hold balance, quick tap to pop out. Keyboard: space or enter = ollie/pop, arrows or WASD = tricks and grind balance.
Loop: procedural street (stairs, rails, ledges, gaps, hydrants, cones, traffic) auto-scrolls; clean landings build speed, sketchy ones bleed it, tricks and grinds chain a combo multiplier that banks on a clean landing.
Fail: mid-rotation touchdowns, lost balance, gaps and obstacles cause a ragdoll bail that ends the run and posts trick score + distance.
Best score persists in localStorage ("curbside.best"); tap the game-over card to restart instantly.
