# Curbside - endless street skating (portrait, Phaser 3 + GGKit)
Controls: HOLD to crouch and RELEASE to ollie, so the pop scales with the squash; in the air swipe LEFT / RIGHT / UP / DOWN = Gutter Whip / Backspin Curl / Skylatch Grab / Drop Shove, and each has a bigger chained variant that only comes out with the hang time to finish it. Swipe UP over a rail, ledge or high line = grind, then micro-drag left and right to hold the wobble meter centred, and hold-release to pop out. Keyboard: space or enter = crouch and pop, arrows or WASD = tricks and grind balance.
Loop: pick a run on the street map (Score Attack, Gap Challenge or Line Run, five districts, medals unlocking the next link in the chain). The procedural street (stairs, rails, ledges, gaps, hydrants, cones, traffic, discoverable high lines) auto-scrolls; clean landings build speed and bank the combo, sketchy ones bleed it, and the multiplier visibly decays if you stall between tricks.
Fail: mid-rotation touchdowns, a lost grind balance, gaps and solid obstacles cause a ragdoll bail that ends the run. Clipping a kerb or ledge face is a scuff, not a bail, and a Bail Save token forgives one outright.
Progress persists through GGKit's guarded save (key "gg-curbside"): medals, bests and the unlocked deck liveries.

## AAA rebuild

Rebuilt in place 2026-08-10 to the AAA fleet bar. The archived prototype
(`engine.js` / `world.js` / the old `game.js`, canvas + vanilla JS) is gone;
the concept it carried is not. Endless skate, procedural auto-scrolling
street, ollie / trick / grind chaining that banks on a clean landing, and a
ragdoll bail fail state all survive, rebuilt on Phaser 3 from
`/play/_shared/` with GGKit as the sole lifecycle, input, save and audio
implementation.

### 1. Mechanics

- **Physical ollie with anticipation.** Hold to crouch: the rig squashes
  (scaleY down, scaleX up) in proportion to charge over 0.32 s, the rider
  pose swaps to `crouch`, and release pops with `POP_MIN + charge *
  POP_RANGE`. A pop out of a grind is its own, flatter impulse.
- **Air tricks on swipe direction.** Left / right / up / down map to eight
  tricks (four base, four chained variants that only come out with the air
  to finish them and a live combo). `air` in the trick table is the single
  authored truth: angular velocity is derived as `spin * TAU / air`, so the
  rotation arc always takes exactly as long as the table says and the
  air-budget check that picked the trick is meaningful. A hop with no room
  for a rotation yields the no-spin grab instead of a doomed flip.
- **The catch.** If the street rises early, the rider may hurry a rotation
  by up to `CATCH_MAX` (1.9x) to land it. Past that the trick genuinely does
  not fit, and the landing judge is entitled to call it.
- **Grind balance as an active skill.** Swipe up in the air over a rail,
  ledge or high-line deck to lock on (26 px snap band, 0.55 s arm window).
  Wobble grows with time on the rail, board speed and the trick's own drift
  (50-50 through Blunt Slide); the only counter is a live horizontal
  micro-drag. A visible wobble meter rides above the skater, turning red and
  pulsing past 0.62 and ticking `sfx_wobble`. Reach 1.0 and you bail.
- **Combo multiplier that visibly decays.** Each trick or grind adds to the
  chain and refills a 2.4 s decay bar on the HUD chip. Stall and the
  multiplier steps DOWN one notch at a time, with a chip pop on each step,
  rather than silently vanishing. Combo Extend pickups freeze the decay.
- **Landing quality by board angle.** Clean under 0.34 rad, sketchy under
  0.92, bail beyond. Clean banks the whole combo at the multiplier and adds
  speed; sketchy keeps the chain alive but banks nothing and bleeds 62 px/s.
- **Scuff tier.** A ledge or kerb FACE is terrain, not an obstacle: clipping
  one costs speed and the combo but never ends the run, at any height. Only
  solid street furniture and traffic bail you. The first build bailed on a
  40 px marble face and the opening seconds of a district felt like a trap.

### 2. Gameplay loop

Fifteen challenges, five districts x three run structures, unlocked strictly
in list order; finishing a run at all opens the next link, so the chain
cannot dead-end on a hard medal. The last entry is the finale line.

| Mode | Structure | Medal metric | Tiers (Downtown -> Mile) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score Attack | 90 s, pure combo chase | final score | 5k/14k/30k -> 11k/32k/68k |
| Gap Challenge | 100 s, named gap list; generation switches to a 3-chunk signature cadence and seeds named street gaps between chunks | gaps cleared | 2/4/6 -> 4/6/9 |
| Line Run | fixed authored sequence, no RNG, no timer; beat markers must be satisfied in their zones | beats landed | 3/5/6 -> 5/7/9 |

Escalation: obstacle and traffic density ramp over the first 900 m, cruise
speed climbs 11 px/s per 100 m, and clutter is only ever placed inside a
flat run the generator laid itself (the first cut dropped a trash can onto
the landing of the chunk that had just ended). **Drops stay generous at every
density**: a 78% chance of a pickup and a 20% chance of a Bail Save token per
chunk, plus three rewards on every discovered shortcut. Combo Extend freezes
decay 4.5 s, Speed Boost adds 168 px/s for 3.2 s, Bonus Trick calls a named
trick worth 2.5x for 4 s, Bail Save forgives one bail with a banner.

### 3. Street design

| District | Identity | Signature gap | Shortcut | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Downtown | violet neon, taxi traffic, stair gauntlet | COURTHOUSE 14 (14-stair, 900) | AWNING LINE (172 px deck over the sets) | stairs + handrails, kerb steps, hydrants, cones, barriers, bank ledges |
| Rail Yard | rust and amber, industrial, no room to coast | LOADING DOCK LEAP (230 px off a raised edge, 1200) | BOXCAR ROOF (190 px deck) | long steel, twin rails at split heights, dock slopes, pallets, crates, trucks |
| Plaza | teal marble, calm, crowd-heavy | FOUNTAIN GAP (200 px off the fountain lip, 1050) | LEDGE RIBBON (150 px, 620 long) | low manual pads, marble hubbas, benches, planters, bollards |
| Boardwalk | pink neon night, fast and airborne | PIER BREAK (300 px off a 66 px kicker, 1500) | RAIL PIER (182 px deck) | kicker ramps, double gaps, pier rails, palms, stalls |
| The Curbside Mile | gold finale, every vocabulary in order | THE MILE DROP (330 px, 2000) | ROOFTOP MILE (205 px deck) | remix: gauntlet, steel, air, marble, traffic |

Each district ships eight to nine authored chunks. The signature chunk is
pinned to every 5th chunk (every 3rd in Gap Challenge) and the shortcut
chunk to every 7th, so a run always shows its centrepiece and its high line.
The first chunk of any run is pinned to the district's gentle opener, after
a 1600 px open run-in. Finding a shortcut pays 750 and a banner.

### 4. Presentation

Authored skater silhouette: eight hand-placed joint tables drawn as capsule
limbs with a neon rim light down the leading edge, anchored to the deck line
inside the pose art so a rolling pose plants its shoes on the plank while a
tuck lifts them off. Five board liveries unlock on medal count. Six pooled
particle systems: wheel/landing dust, grind chalk, grind sparks, bail smoke,
pickup glow, gap shock ring. Six-part ragdoll plus a tumbling deck on bail.
Parallax skyline per district (two tileable strips, seam cross-faded) with
crowd silhouettes swaying behind the street. Big beats use a 60%-width
banner with an overshoot settle. Audio is on GGKit's buses: three synthesised
music beds that carry the street-ambience traffic layer themselves, plus
nineteen cues; wheel roll and grind scrape are granulated on the SFX bus at a
rate and pitch tied to board speed, which keeps the single music channel free
for the district bed. Reduced motion is gated throughout: no shake, no
overshoot, no bob or sway, reduced particle counts, faster result card.

### 5. Known bug classes, and what was done about them

Every class named in the brief, plus the two the feel pass turned up.

1. **Debug views separate from preallocated pools.** `__cb.state.livePickups`
   is its own 16-record array, copied by value each frame. It can never
   alias or truncate a sim pool.
2. **Per-entity render state on the entity.** Sim records hold no sprite
   handles. Sprites are leased in immediate mode each frame (`leaseSprite` /
   `finishPool`), so the renderer reads records and writes nothing.
3. **DOM control handlers must seed `kit.input.pointers`.** This title ships
   no DOM gameplay control; all play input reads GGKit's pointer identity map
   and key set. `window.__cbSeedPointer` exists as the required entry point
   if one is ever added.
4. **Camera splits must create the second camera.** One camera, never split.
5. **Phaser plain-config scenes need `extend:`.** All three scenes are plain
   objects with their methods under `extend`.
6. **Test switches readable from the boot fallback AND the live scene.**
   `CB_DEBUG_STATE` is built at module scope, published immediately, and only
   ever mutated - never replaced - so a switch set before `Play` exists is
   still honoured, in the menu as well as in a run.
7. **No clock past the stepped sim.** `stepSim()` is a fixed 60 Hz
   accumulator capped at 5 steps; leftover time is discarded, so a degraded
   device runs in slow motion and never time-skips. The cosmetic clock is
   separate and is the only thing hit-stop freezes.
8. **Guarded keyed lookups.** Every district, mode, trick, grind, pickup,
   livery and challenge lookup goes through a `CB_DATA` resolver that falls
   back to the first table entry. Persisted ids are validated against the
   live registries on load.
9. **Coach UI is a thin fading strip** under the top HUD band at ~24% screen
   height, clear of the pause control and of the banner. It never covers the
   play centre or the bottom half.
10. **`sw.js` precaches only files that exist** - the list is generated from
    the real directory listing and re-verified against the filesystem.

Two more, found in this build and fixed:

11. **Per-frame allocation in the render path.** The first street renderer
    built a fresh `{x,y}` array per polygon and re-`map`ped the medal array
    sixty times a second. Both are now preallocated.
12. **Static `Graphics` is not free.** A Phaser Graphics object re-submits
    its whole command list every frame, and a rounded rectangle is a
    tessellated path. Baking every static panel and gradient into a cached
    tinted texture took the 4x-throttle menu from a 33.4 ms median to 16.7.

### 6. Verification

`window.__cb = { state, forceEvent(), forceDistrict(), forceMode(), version }`.
`state` carries `mode`, `modeName`, `district`, `districtName`,
`districtIndex`, `score`, `combo`, `multiplier`, `distance`, `speed`,
`riderState`, `landing`, `balance`, `grinding`, `wobbleShown`, `airTime`,
`timeLeft`, `gapsCleared`, `gapNames`, `beatsHit`/`beatsTotal`,
`shortcutsFound`, `saves`, `boost`, `prompt`, `livePickups` +
`livePickupCount`, `medals`, `unlockedCount`, `bestScore`, `over`, `result`,
`medalIndex`, `lastBanner`, `lastEvent`, `recentEvents` (12-deep trail), and
`reducedMotion`.

Switches, honoured from the menu and mid-run: `forceDistrict`, `forceMode`,
`forceUnlockAll`, `forceGenerousDrops`, and `forceEvent` accepting `bail`,
`save`, `gap`, `shortcut`, `combo`, `bank`, `prompt`, `boost`, `grind`,
`wobble`, `banner`, `over`. `forceEvent('grind')` snaps the skater onto the
next rail ahead and enters the balance state, so the wobble meter can be
verified without a lucky swipe.

**Ran:** `node --check` on `cb_data.js`, `cb_world.js`, `game.js`, `sw.js`
(all clean) and `JSON.parse` on `manifest.json`. Headless sim harness across
all five districts x three modes. Browser probe
(`aaa/harness/cb_probe.mjs`) - boot, menu, run, all twelve forced events,
result card, unlock chain, reduced-motion pass, and every district/mode
combination: **zero console errors, zero failed requests.** Fleet gate
(`aaa/harness/gate.mjs`): http 200, viewport 390, non-black frame, colour
richness 351 (menu) / 210 (play), feel median **16.7 ms under 4x throttle**,
payload **1008 KB**, largest file **118 KB**, manifest and icons present.

**Could not run / known deviations:**

- `feel_no_spikes` (budget 6/600) does not pass on this machine. Repeat runs
  gave 17, 28, 47, 76, 97 and 129 frames over 33 ms out of 600, in that
  spread, from an identical build - the number swings by 8x run to run,
  which is contention, not content. The median is 16.7 ms in every run and
  the un-throttled trace is spotless (0/300, p99 16.8 ms). The accepted
  flagship peer `skyfall-command` measures 134/600 on this same machine in
  the same session. This needs a re-run on an uncontended box for a reading
  worth trusting; the best observed run here is 17/600.
- `pwa_sw` fails because service workers only register over https; the probe
  runs on `http://localhost`. `sw.js` itself is authored, syntax-checked and
  its precache list verified against the filesystem.
- Deployment is out of scope for this lane: nothing was deployed and nothing
  was committed.

### 7. Deferred

- The four district beds share one 148/124 BPM pair. Per-district stems, and
  an intensity layer that fades in above a speed threshold, are the obvious
  next audio pass.
- Shortcut high lines are discovered by riding them; there is no map or
  post-run summary of which ones a district still hides.
- Manual and revert tricks are absent. The ledge "manual pad" chunks in
  Plaza currently read as short low ledges.
- The bail ragdoll is six parts with no joint constraints, so it tumbles
  rather than articulating.
- Line Run beats are satisfied anywhere inside a 340 px zone; a stricter
  ordering check (beat N must land after beat N-1) is not enforced.
- Liveries are cosmetic only; no deck stats, and no rider customisation.
- Crowd reaction is ambient sway only - it does not respond to a big gap
  clear or a bail.
