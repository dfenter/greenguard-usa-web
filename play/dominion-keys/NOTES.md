# Dominion Keys
Controls: drag a brass key handle outward (or tap it); keyboard: arrows select, Space/Enter pulls, R retries, Esc toggles the kingdom.
Loop: 30 chambers over 3 wings. Pull keys in the right order so treasure falls to the hero's well, lava drains into the side pits, and beasts land in the lava.
Hazards combine: water freezes lava to stone, marshgas ignites into fire on contact with lava.
Every rescue adds a villager to the kingdom screen and unlocks the next chamber; clearing all 30 earns the crown. Progress and fewest-keys bests are saved locally.
Each chamber was validated by exhaustive pull-order search; a pull that makes the chamber unwinnable fails instantly so you can retry.
