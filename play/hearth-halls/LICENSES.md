# Hearth & Halls licenses

## Original title work

All room art, furniture silhouettes, tile symbols, character designs, UI, and visual effects in this directory are procedural original work authored in `game.js`. No external art or audio files are shipped by the title.

The title follows the Puzzle Pop lane guidance in `play/_assets/ART_puzzlepop.md`. The permitted asset inventory and provenance rules are recorded in `play/_assets/LEDGER.md`; this rebuild uses no harvested pack files, so there are no additional file-level asset credits.

## Shared runtime

`/play/_shared/phaser.min.js` and `/play/_shared/ggkit.js` are shared GreenGuard studio runtime files. Their licensing and provenance are documented in `play/_shared/LICENSES.md`.

## Audio

The `assets/audio/` MP3 files are original synthesized tones and simple chord loops generated for this title. They are registered through GGKit under the `music-home`, `music-board`, `tap`, `select`, `invalid`, `swap-tick`, `match-chime`, `cascade`, `hint`, `goal`, `reveal-sting`, `character-vocal`, `room-complete`, `ui-confirm`, and `comfort-place` bus names. No external audio is used.
