Why Wineries Cannot Use Conventional Sprays

The pollinator and food-safety problem

Wineries occupy a unique position in the outdoor protection market: they depend on pollinators for their core agricultural product, they serve food and beverages in outdoor settings, and they attract guests specifically to experience the natural environment. Conventional mosquito spray programs conflict with all three of these.

Synthetic pyrethroids applied to winery grounds land on vine leaves and soil, affect the bee populations that support vineyard health, and leave residue on outdoor tasting and event surfaces. For organically certified and sustainably managed vineyards, conventional spray programs are simply not an option. GreenGuard USA is.

Purpose-Built for Winery Environments
