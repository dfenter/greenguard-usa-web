The Opportunity

A persistent problem.
A better solution.

Mosquitoes are a year-round problem in warm climates, and consumer demand for pesticide-free alternatives is growing fast. GreenGuard USA was built to meet that shift.

Growing Consumer Demand

Homeowners increasingly question chemical sprays around children, pets, and pollinators. GreenGuard USA's pesticide-free positioning resonates strongly with modern buyers and commands premium pricing.

Built-In Repeat Business

CO₂ traps require refills every 20–27 days. Every customer you install becomes a recurring monthly service account, creating revenue continuity that supports planning and growth.

Clear Market Differentiation

GreenGuard USA is not a commodity spray service. The technology-forward, eco-responsible positioning sets franchisees apart from traditional pest control, supporting premium pricing and retention.

Lean to Start, Built to Scale

Many franchisees begin with a small team and limited equipment. As demand grows, vehicles and technicians can be added incrementally, no need to restructure the business as you scale.

Bee-Safe & Pollinator-Friendly

CO₂ trapping targets only blood-seeking insects. This differentiator resonates with gardeners, beekeepers, HOAs, and environmentally conscious communities, a built-in marketing advantage.

Protected Territories

GreenGuard USA awards territories selectively to protect market opportunity. Early franchisees benefit from strong positioning as the brand expands nationally.

Revenue Model

Multiple streams.
All recurring.

The GreenGuard USA model is structured around ongoing monthly service, not one-time transactions. Every customer installation creates a long-term revenue relationship.

Monthly CO₂ Tank Delivery

Every trap customer needs a CO₂ refill every 20–27 days. Scheduled delivery routes create predictable monthly billing at $99–$639 per customer.

Full-Service Trap Rentals

Rental customers pay a higher monthly rate that includes the trap, CO₂, attractant refills, and all maintenance, the highest revenue-per-customer tier.

Trap & Equipment Sales

One-time equipment sales to customers who prefer ownership. These customers then become CO₂ delivery subscribers, layering recurring revenue on top.

Commercial & HOA Accounts

Restaurants, event venues, HOA common areas, and commercial properties represent high-value multi-unit accounts with strong retention.
