Still Have Questions?

We've heard every concern.

"Will it actually work on my property?"

CO₂ trapping has been scientifically validated across millions of deployments worldwide, with 87, 93% bite reduction in independent studies. If our free consultation determines your property isn't a good fit for a trap, we'll tell you, we'd rather be honest than sign you up for something that won't perform.

"How long before I notice a difference?"

Most customers notice significantly fewer bites within the first 7, 10 days. Within 2, 3 weeks, the mosquito breeding cycle begins to collapse, meaning you're not just repelling mosquitoes, you're reducing the total population around your property.

"What if I want to cancel?"

We operate month-to-month with no long-term contracts. If you want to stop, just let us know. We'll come collect the equipment with no penalty or hassle. We keep customers because the service works, not because they're locked in.

"Is it safe around my kids, dogs, and garden?"

Completely. CO₂ traps use zero pesticides, pesticides, or sprays. They target only blood-seeking female mosquitoes. Bees, butterflies, pets, and children are entirely safe around both the Biogents and Mosqitter Grand traps at all times.

"What happens if the trap breaks or needs service?"

It's our equipment, our problem. Any maintenance, repairs, or replacements are covered as part of your rental. You'll never pay extra for a service visit. Just contact us and we handle it, typically within 24, 48 hours.

"Do I need to do anything day-to-day?"

Nothing. The trap runs continuously and silently. Your CO₂ arrives monthly. The only thing you might do is check the catch net occasionally, or opt into the full-service rental where we handle that too.
