spBv1.0/sparkbridge/NDATA/why-sparkbridge

Why Teams Switch

Six capabilities we could not find in the commercial modules' published documentation.

Scale

Shared-subscription scale-out

Four consumers share one broker stream and deliver about 20x the ingest of one. We have found no competing Ignition module that removes the single-subscriber ceiling.

Speed

Published, measured numbers

Load and endurance scenarios you can run yourself, with the hardware stated. No competing module publishes comparable figures.

Uptime

Change settings without a restart

Configuration hot-reloads in about five seconds. No scheduled outage to add a site or change a deadband.

Bandwidth

Metadata folding

Units, ranges and descriptions ride the birth certificate instead of the data stream, taking roughly a third of points out of steady-state traffic.

Security

Per-site scoping as standard

An edge credential is locked to its own site, cannot issue commands, and cannot write another site's data. Proven live against the broker.

Licensing

No client-count tiers

The broker is not licensed by how many clients connect, so there is no 50 or 250 ceiling to design around.
