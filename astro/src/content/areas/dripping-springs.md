## Dripping Springs Mosquito Hotspots

**Onion Creek headwaters** properties experience the highest sustained natural pressure. The creek's karst-fed flow maintains moisture even through dry periods, sustaining large breeding populations near the water.

**Belterra and Caliterra** new development neighborhoods have the retention pond and drainage infrastructure of suburban construction combined with the rural landscape of Hays County, creating unusually high pressure for newer neighborhoods.

**RR-12 and US-290 corridor** event venues, wineries, and businesses face commercial pressure that requires professional-grade solutions beyond homeowner-level treatments.

**Driftwood** rural properties with stock tanks, native plant gardens, and low-lying land accumulate standing water across large acreage, sustaining floodwater mosquito species uncommon in the suburbs.

## Dripping Springs Mosquito Season

Season runs March through November. Rural Hays County properties can see shorter peaks due to faster drainage, but event venues and irrigated properties face sustained pressure identical to urban Austin.
