If you own a CO2 mosquito trap like the Biogents BG-Mosquitaire, the trap itself is the easy part. The recurring question is where to get the CO2 tank filled once it runs empty. Here is the practical answer for Austin.

## What kind of tank and fill you need

Most CO2 mosquito traps use a **20 lb aluminum CO2 cylinder with a CGA-320 valve**, the same standard used for beverage and homebrew CO2. That is good news, because it means a lot of places around Austin can fill it.

You have two options at most shops:

- **Refill:** they fill your actual tank while you wait or leave it. Cheapest, but you are without CO2 for a bit.
- **Exchange:** you swap your empty for an already-full tank and leave. Faster, sometimes slightly more.

## Where to get a CO2 fill in Austin

Any of these can fill a CGA-320 cylinder:

- **Welding supply stores** (the most reliable option for a 20 lb tank).
- **Homebrew and beer-making shops.**
- **Fire extinguisher service companies.**
- **Paintball and sporting goods shops** (usually smaller tanks, call first for 20 lb).

Call ahead to confirm they fill 20 lb CGA-320 cylinders, since some locations only handle smaller sizes.

## What it costs

A 20 lb CO2 fill in the Austin area typically runs **$20 to $35**. Price varies by shop and by whether you refill or exchange.

## How long a tank lasts

- **Running continuously:** a 20 lb tank lasts roughly 20 days.
- **On an 8-hour daily timer:** the same tank stretches to about 60 days, because mosquitoes hunt most at dawn and dusk anyway.

A CO2 timer is the single best way to cut your refill trips. It can triple how long each tank lasts.
