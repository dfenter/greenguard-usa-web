The Revenue Case for Outdoor Protection

What mosquito pressure costs your patio

A table that stays for two hours in a comfortable outdoor environment versus forty-five minutes because guests are uncomfortable represents a meaningful revenue difference across a full season of outdoor service. Most guests will not say they are leaving because of mosquitoes. They simply leave earlier.

Restaurant operators who have addressed the mosquito problem consistently report the same outcome: guests stay longer, order more, and come back more often. The outdoor dining experience becomes a competitive advantage rather than a liability.
