## Kyle Mosquito Hotspots

**Plum Creek corridor** runs through the heart of Kyle's older development and provides the most consistent natural breeding pressure in the city. The creek-side trail and park areas attract significant tiger mosquito populations.

**6 Creeks and Anthem** newer master-planned communities have the retention infrastructure and amenity centers common to large developments, concentrating pressure throughout community common areas.

**Waterleaf and Carriage Hills** neighborhoods have the irrigation and landscape density of established suburban development combined with the creek-adjacent geography that sustains natural pressure.

**I-35 corridor** commercial development with ornamental landscaping and detention basins creates sustained pressure that bleeds into adjacent residential areas.

## Kyle Mosquito Season

Season: April through October. Kyle's Blackland Prairie soils create slightly extended post-rain breeding windows compared to Hill Country locations. Late-season thunderstorms can produce late-season pressure spikes into November.
