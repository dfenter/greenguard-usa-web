Georgetown is Williamson County's county seat and one of its most diverse mosquito pressure environments, combining an urban river corridor, a major reservoir, one of the largest active adult communities in the country, and rapidly growing suburban development all within one city.

## Georgetown Mosquito Hotspots

**San Gabriel River corridor** through historic Georgetown provides the most consistent natural pressure. Properties near the river park system, Blue Hole, and the downtown Rivery area experience Culex activity throughout warm months.

**Lake Georgetown** waterfront and north Georgetown properties near the Corps of Engineers recreation area face sustained pressure from the reservoir's breeding zones during spring and summer.

**Sun City Georgetown** with its 5,000+ acres of golf courses, retention ponds, and irrigated common areas creates a self-sustaining mosquito environment that individual homeowners within the community cannot address alone.

**Wolf Ranch and Parkside on the River** newer developments have created suburban infrastructure adjacent to the San Gabriel River, combining natural and built breeding pressure.

## Georgetown Mosquito Season

Season: March through November. Georgetown's position slightly north of Austin proper gives it marginally shorter seasons, but pressure during core months is equivalent to Travis County.
