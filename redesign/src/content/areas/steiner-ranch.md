Steiner Ranch residents invest heavily in outdoor living (patios, pools, waterfront access, trail systems), but without professional mosquito control, those investments go underused from May through October. CO2 trapping creates the pressure-free zone those spaces need.

## Steiner Ranch Mosquito Hotspots

**Lake Austin waterfront** sections of Steiner Ranch face the highest pressure from Culex species breeding in the lake's coves, marinas, and shoreline drainage. Evening pressure here can be severe.

**Greenbelt trail-adjacent properties** experience daily tiger mosquito pressure from the shaded, humid trail corridors running through the community. Properties backing to the greenbelt are in the highest risk zone.

**HOA retention ponds** throughout Steiner Ranch are maintained for aesthetics but function as breeding reservoirs. Properties within 300 feet of any retention feature see elevated background pressure even without other breeding sites.

**Quinlan Park Road corridor** and community pool areas have irrigation and shade that creates tiger mosquito habitat throughout the neighborhood's common spaces.

## Steiner Ranch Mosquito Season

Season: March through November, with waterfront properties seeing active pressure from late February. The lake's thermal mass extends both the start and end of the season compared to inland Austin neighborhoods.
