HOW TO PLAY

Tap a unit to select it. Move highlights reachable slabs, and tap a slab to walk. Choose Attack or Ability, then tap a target twice to confirm. Drag with one pointer to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, and use the wheel or pinch to zoom. Hold a unit for 450ms to center the camera. Skip is available during a presentation.
